# Security

KitStak holds our customers' orders, inventory, contacts and billing. If you find a hole, please tell
us first.

## Reporting a problem

Email **hello@kitstak.com** with "Security" in the subject line. Please don't open a public GitHub
issue or pull request for anything that could be exploited.

It helps a lot if you include:

- which app it affects (Portal, CRM, WMS, Projects, Pulse or kitstak.com)
- what you did, step by step, and what happened
- what an attacker could get or change
- any proof of concept, request logs or screenshots

## What happens next

- We'll reply within two business days to say we've got it.
- We'll keep you posted while we look into it and fix it.
- Once a fix is out, we're happy to credit you if you'd like.

## Please don't

- touch data that isn't yours, or keep any you come across by accident
- run anything that slows down or breaks the service for other people
- try phishing or social engineering on our team or customers

If you stick to that and give us a reasonable chance to fix things before you share them, we won't
treat your research as a violation.

## Supported versions

KitStak is a hosted service. Only what is running now is supported: the apps at kitstak.app and the
site at kitstak.com. Fixes land on `main` and deploy from there.
