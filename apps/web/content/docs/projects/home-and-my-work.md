**Home** is your start page: your numbers, what is due, your inbox and your goals. **My work** lists every task assigned to you across every project, sorted by when it is due. Start your day on either one.

## Home

Go to **Home** in the sidebar, or press `G` then `H`.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting | Good morning, afternoon or evening, with your name |
| **Your numbers** | **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Open** and **Done this week** |
| **When it’s due** | Your tasks due today, overdue, and in the next 7 days |
| **Inbox** | Your latest unread notifications |
| **Goals** | Up to four goals, yours first, then other top goals |
| **Recent projects** | Projects you opened lately |
| Customers card | With CRM or WMS, deal stages and warehouse receipts and exceptions for customers linked to your projects |
| **Programs today** | With programs, today's builds and output for each running program |
| Getting started | The checklist for new people. See [KitStak Projects overview](/docs/projects/overview) |

When a list is empty, select **Add a task for today** to add a task due today. When nothing is due, Home says so, such as **Nothing due today** or **Nothing overdue**. Select **See all** to open My work.

## My work

Go to **My work** in the sidebar, or press `G` then `M`.

### Pick which tasks

| Control | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Which tasks** | **Assigned to me**, **Created by me** or **Following** |
| **Group by** | **By due date** or **By project** |
| **Sort by** | **Due date**, **Priority**, **Project**, **Recently updated**, **Name (A to Z)** |
| **Project** | **All projects** or one project |
| **Filter tasks** | Finds tasks by words |
| **Show done** | Shows tasks you finished |
| **Show builds** | With programs, lists your builds for today and earlier, by run |

My work keeps your choices in the page address, so a link opens the same list.

### The due date groups

| Group | Tasks in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue** | Past their due date |
| **Today** | Due today |
| **Upcoming** | Due in the next 7 days |
| **Later** | Due after next week |
| **No date** | Not scheduled yet |
| **Done lately** | Finished in the last 7 days, with **Show done** on |

### Reschedule and finish tasks

- Drag a task into another group to change its due date. The **Today** group is always there: drag a task into it, or press `T` on one, to do it today.
- Select the round check to mark a task done. **Undo** brings it back.
- Use `J` and `K` to move, `Enter` to open, and the task keys from [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/projects/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Focus mode

Focus mode shows one task at a time from today's and overdue work, most urgent first.

1. In My work, select **Focus mode** at the top, or press `F`. It shows how many tasks are waiting. The button is off when nothing is due today or overdue.
2. For each task, pick **Mark done** (`Ctrl` `Enter`), **Do it tomorrow** (`Shift` `T`), **Skip** (`N`) or **Open details** (`Enter`).

The bar at the bottom shows how far through your list you are. When you finish, it says **You cleared your list!**. With nothing due today or overdue, Focus mode says **Nothing to focus on**.
