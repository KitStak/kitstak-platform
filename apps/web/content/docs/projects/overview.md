KitStak Projects is where your team plans work, sees who is doing what, and hits its dates. It works on its own, or together with KitStak CRM and KitStak WMS. Every project has its own statuses, sections, labels and custom fields, and six views of the same tasks.

## Before you start

- Your organization needs Projects in its plan. See [pricing](/pricing) or [contact us](/contact).
- You need a KitStak sign-in. It is the same sign-in you use for the other KitStak apps. See [getting started](/docs/getting-started).

## Sign in

1. Go to `projects.kitstak.app`.
2. Enter your **Work email** and **Password**.
3. Tick **Keep me signed in** if you are on your own computer.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If you are not in an organization yet, Projects shows **You are not in an organization yet** with a **Join an organization** link. Ask a workspace admin to invite you from the KitStak Portal.

## Find your way around

The left sidebar holds the main pages.

| Sidebar item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A greeting, your numbers, what is due, your inbox and goals |
| **My work** | Every task assigned to you across all projects |
| **Inbox** | Your notifications, with a count of unread ones |
| **Projects** | Every project you can see, as cards or a table |
| **Programs** | Repeat work for your customers, with a count of running programs |
| **Floor today** | Today's builds across every program, shown while a program is running |
| **Goals** | Objectives with key results and check-ins |
| **Dashboards** | Reports for one project or all of them |
| **Automations** | Rules that do routine work for you |
| **Templates** | Ready-made project plans |
| **Forms** | Intake forms that turn answers into tasks |
| **Settings** | Your profile, appearance, workspace and integrations |

Below these, the sidebar lists **Starred** projects and programs, a **Programs** section, and **Spaces** with the open projects in each space. Projects with no space are listed under **Other projects**. Completed and archived projects leave the sidebar; you find them on the **Projects** page.

Select **Collapse sidebar** at the bottom, or press `[`, to shrink the sidebar to a row of icons.

## The top bar

- **Search** opens the command palette. See [search and the command palette](/docs/projects/search-and-command-palette).
- **New task** opens quick add. The arrow next to it, **More ways to create**, offers **New task**, **New build**, **New project**, **New program**, **New form** and **New automation**. Workspace admins also see **Invite people**.
- The bell opens your **Inbox**.
- **Help** offers **Getting started**, **Take the tour**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Browse templates** and **KitStak Portal**.
- The account menu offers **My settings**, **Notification settings**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **All KitStak apps**, **Workspace settings** and **Sign out**.

## The welcome tour

The first time you open Projects, a welcome dialog appears. Pick one:

- **Show me around** starts a short tour of the sidebar, quick add, search and My work.
- **Try a sample project** creates a sample project from the Product launch template and opens its board. Its first four dated tasks are assigned to you. Delete it whenever you like.
- **I’ll look around myself** closes the dialog.

To replay the tour later, open **Help** and select **Take the tour**.

## The getting started checklist

Home shows a checklist until you finish it or hide it. Each step ticks itself off when you do it.

| Step | How it is done |
| --- | --- |
| **Start a project** | You can see at least one project |
| **Add your first task** | You create a task |
| **Give a task a due date** | One of your tasks has a due date |
| **Check off a task** | One of your tasks is done |
| **Find anything fast** | You use search |
| **Bring your team** | There is more than one person in the workspace |
| **Set up a program** | Optional, shown when there are no programs yet |

Select **Hide checklist** to put it away. Bring it back from **Help > Getting started**.
