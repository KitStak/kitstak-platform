The **Settings** page holds your own settings and the workspace's. Open it from **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, or from the account menu (**My settings**, **Notification settings**, **Workspace settings**). Pick a section on the left.

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Name, password, notifications |
| **Appearance** | Light or dark, sidebar size |
| **Workspace** | Plan, spaces and people |
| **Integrations** | Portal, CRM and WMS |
| **Keyboard shortcuts** | Work without the mouse |
| **Work calendar** | Work days, holidays, capacity |

## Profile

1. Under **Your profile**, change **Your name** and save. Teammates will see it everywhere in KitStak. Your **Email** and workspace role show here too.
2. Under **Password**, enter your **Current password**, a **New password** and **New password again**. Tick **Sign me out on my other devices** if you want to.
3. Under **Inbox notifications**, turn each kind of notification on or off. See [inbox and notifications](/docs/projects/inbox).

The new password must be different from the current one, and both new boxes must match.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: **Light** (bright and clear), **Dark** (easy on the eyes at night) or **Match my device**. Your choice is shared with the Portal, CRM and WMS on this device.
- **Small sidebar**: shows the sidebar as a row of icons. You can also press `[`.

You can also switch with **Dark mode** and **Light mode** in the command palette.

## Workspace

The **Workspace** panel shows how many people are in the workspace, with **Invite or manage people** and **Plan and billing**. Both open the KitStak Portal.

### Spaces

Spaces group projects by team or department, such as Operations or Marketing.

1. Under **Spaces**, select **Add space**.
2. Enter the **Name** and **What happens here?**
3. Pick a **Color** and **Icon**.
4. Select **Add space**.

Pick a space when you make a project, or move projects into it from their settings. To change a space, open its menu and select **Edit**. To delete one, select **Delete** then **Delete space**. Its projects stay and move to **Other projects** in the sidebar. Members and above can manage spaces.

## Integrations

The **Integrations** section shows each KitStak app and what it adds to Projects.

| App | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| KitStak Portal | One sign-in for every app, invite people once and use them everywhere, open tasks and at-risk projects on your launcher, and switching apps from the grid at the top |
| KitStak CRM | Link tasks and projects to deals and accounts, see the deal's stage right on the task, start a project from a deal, and automations that run when a deal changes stage |
| KitStak WMS | Link tasks to orders, work orders, receipts and counts, see order status on the task, the Warehouse go-live and Client onboarding templates, and automations that run when an order ships |

An app your organization doesn't have shows **Not in your plan**. Admins see a link to add it in the Portal; others are asked to ask an admin. **Shared clients** shows how many records of each kind are linked, such as **Linked deals and accounts** and **Linked orders**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

The **Keyboard shortcuts** section lists every shortcut. Use **Find a shortcut** to search. See [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/projects/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Work calendar

The work calendar sets the work days and holidays for every program that uses the workspace calendar.

1. Go to **Settings** and select **Work calendar**.
2. Pick the **Time zone**. Program days start and end at midnight here.
3. Under **Work days**, pick the days the floor works.
4. Optional: set **Daily capacity** in hours a day. Floor today and Workload show planned hours against it.
5. Under **Holidays**, select **Add holiday** and pick the date and name, such as `Thanksgiving`. Or select **Copy last year's holidays**.
6. Select **Save calendar**, or **Discard changes**.

Schedules skip holidays and forecasts leave them out. Changes apply to future builds and forecasts; logged output does not change. Owners and admins can change the calendar; everyone else can view it.
