Your **Inbox** collects notifications: someone mentioned you, gave you a task, commented on a task you follow, or a task of yours is due. The sidebar shows how many are unread. You never get a notification about a change you made yourself.

## Open the inbox

- Select **Inbox** in the sidebar, or the bell in the top bar.
- Or press `G` then `I`.

## Filter notifications

Pick a filter at the top.

| Filter | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everything that is not archived |
| **Unread** | What you have not read yet |
| **Mentions** | Someone typed @ and your name in a comment |
| **Assigned** | Tasks someone gave you |
| **Comments** | New comments on tasks you follow |
| **Due dates** | Reminders for your tasks due today or tomorrow, or late |
| **Programs** | Missing output, price cards waiting for review, minimums at risk and months ready to close, when you have programs |
| **Archive** | Notifications you archived |

When a filter is empty, it says so, such as **You’re all caught up** or **No mentions yet**.

## Read and act on a notification

1. Select a notification. Its preview opens beside the list, with a link to the task.
2. Use **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread**, and **Archive**. In **Archive**, select **Move to inbox** to bring one back.

To act on many at once, tick them, or press `X`, then use the buttons at the top. **Select all** and **Clear selection** are there too. Select **Mark all read** to clear every unread notification.

### Inbox keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `J` and `K`, or the arrows | Move between notifications |
| `Enter` | Open |
| `E` | Archive |
| `U` | Mark read or unread |
| `X` | Select |
| `Shift` `R` | Mark everything read |

## Due date reminders

Projects adds a reminder to your inbox when a task assigned to you is due today or tomorrow, and when it is past its due date. Reminders show under **Due dates**.

## What you get notified about

| Kind | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Assigned to me** | Someone gives you a task |
| **Mentions** | Someone writes @your name in a comment |
| **Due soon** | Your task is due today or tomorrow |
| **Overdue** | Your task is past its due date |
| **New comments** | Someone comments on a task you follow |
| **Status changes** | A task you follow moves to a new step |
| **Other changes** | Dates, priority or the assignee change on tasks you follow |
| **Project updates** | You’re added to a project, its health changes, or a form gets a response |
| **Automations** | Rules that send you a heads-up |
| **CRM and WMS** | Linked deals and orders change |

You follow tasks you create, tasks assigned to you, and tasks you choose to follow. See [comments, files, followers and time](/docs/projects/comments-files-and-time).

## Choose your notifications

1. Open the account menu and select **Notification settings**. Or go to **Settings > Profile**.
2. Under **Inbox notifications**, turn each kind on or off. They are grouped as **Work for you**, **Tasks you follow** and **Projects and apps**.
3. Use **Turn all on** or **Turn all off** to change them all at once.

Turning everything off makes the inbox quiet. You can still find all your work in **My work**.
