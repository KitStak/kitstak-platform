The **Dashboards** page shows reports for all your active projects together, or for one project. Reports fill in on their own as your team works: there is nothing to set up.

## Before you start

- Reports count top-level tasks in active projects you can see. Archived projects are left out. Subtasks count with their parent task.

## Open reports

1. Go to **Dashboards**, or press `G` then `D`.
2. Under **Show reports for**, pick **All projects** or one project. A project's **Overview** and its menu on the Projects page (**See reports**) open its reports directly.
3. Optional: pick a **Space** to narrow **All projects**.
4. Pick the **Time range**: **4 weeks**, **8 weeks** or **12 weeks**.
5. Select **Refresh** to load the latest numbers.

## The numbers at the top

| Tile | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Open tasks** | Tasks that are not done yet, the same count as the Portal tile |
| **Done this week** | Tasks finished since Monday, compared with all of last week |
| **Overdue** | Open tasks whose due date has passed |
| **Finished per week** | How many tasks the team finishes in a normal week (velocity) |
| **Time to finish** | Median days from when work starts (or the task is created) until it is done (cycle time) |
| **Unassigned** | Open tasks nobody is assigned to yet |

## The report panels

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Burn-up** and **Burn-down** | Pick one with **Chart type**. Burn-up shows **Total work** and **Done**: when the green line meets the blue line, everything is finished. Burn-down shows **Work left**, which should fall toward zero by the due date. Stay under the dashed **On-time pace** line to finish on time |
| **Velocity** | Tasks, or story points, finished each week. Pick **Tasks** or **Points** with **Count** |
| **Time to finish** | The median days to finish, week by week |
| **Where tasks are** | Open tasks by status: **To do**, **In progress**, **Done** and **Overdue** |
| **Workload** | Open tasks per person, including **Unassigned** |
| **Overdue by person** | Overdue tasks per person |
| **Finished this week** | The tasks finished this week, with links to them |

The on-time pace line needs a due date on the project. Without one, burn-down still shows work left.

## Projects at a glance

With **All projects**, the **Projects at a glance** panel lists every active project with its progress ring and health. Select one to see its reports.

## Program reports

When your workspace has programs, Dashboards also shows **Programs vs target**, **Program units by month** and **Program revenue by month**, and **Units by variant**: up to 10 variants with the most units this month, across programs. Pick **Units** or **Revenue** with **Show**. People who can't see money see units only, with **Money is hidden for your role.**
