The **Projects** page lists every project you can see. Its subtitle counts your active projects and says how many are off track, at risk or need a status check. From here you filter, sort, star, copy, archive and delete projects.

## Before you start

- You only see projects that are open to the whole workspace, or private projects you were added to.

## Filter the list

1. Go to **Projects**.
2. Type in **Find a project by name or key**. Press `Esc` to clear it.
3. Narrow the list with the drop-downs and buttons.

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Space** | **All spaces**, one space, or **Other projects** for projects with no space |
| **Status** | **Any status**, **Planning**, **In progress** or **On hold** (on the **Completed** tab: **Done**) |
| **Health** | **Any health**, **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track** |
| **Starred** | Shows only the projects you starred |
| **Show** | **Active**, **Completed** or **Archived** |

Select **Clear filters** to remove them all.

## Change the layout

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Group** | **By space** or **One list** |
| **Layout** | **Cards** or **Table** |
| **Sort by** (cards) | **Needs attention first**, **Due soonest**, **Least progress**, **Name A to Z**, **Recently updated** |

Your layout choices are remembered for next time.

## Read a project's health

Each project shows one health, set by its owner: **On track** (going as planned), **At risk** (might slip without help) or **Off track** (behind and needs attention).

Projects also compares the share of time used, from the start date to the due date, with the share of tasks done. When the numbers disagree with the owner, a small amber dot sits on the health chip with the tip **Numbers suggest** and the health they point to. Select the chip to see why. Editors can update the status from there in one click. The numbers never change the health on their own.

Finished projects and projects that have not started yet have no pace to compare, so they never show the dot.

## Project actions

Open the menu on a card or row to see these actions. What you see depends on your role.

| Action | Who sees it | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Open project** | Everyone | Opens the project |
| **See reports** | Everyone | Opens Dashboards for this project |
| **Star project** | Everyone | Adds it to **Starred** in the sidebar. **Remove star** takes it off |
| **Make a copy** | People who can create projects | Copies the project with its tasks, named with “(copy)”, and opens it |
| **Project settings** | Owners and editors | Opens the project's settings |
| **Archive project** | Owners and editors | Hides it from Projects, the sidebar and reports. **Restore project** brings it back |
| **Delete project** | Owners | Removes it and its tasks for everyone |

Archive, restore and delete show an **Undo** button right after.

## Where completed and archived projects go

- A project whose stage is set to **Done** moves to the **Completed** tab.
- An archived project moves to the **Archived** tab. It is read-only in lists and reports until you restore it.
