The **Calendar** tab shows a project's tasks on the days they happen, in a month or a week. Drag a task to another day to reschedule it, or click an empty spot to add a task due that day.

## Before you start

- To move or add tasks you need the Editor or Owner role on the project.

## Move around the calendar

- Pick **Month** or **Week** with **Calendar view**.
- Use **Previous month** and **Next month** (or **Previous week** and **Next week**) to move.
- Use **Find a task** to find a task by name.
- A day with more tasks than fit shows a **more** link. Select it to see everything on that day.

Tasks past their due date and not done are marked **Overdue**.

## Reschedule a task

- Drag the task to another day. It keeps the same number of days from start to due.
- With a task selected, hold `Alt` and press the arrow keys to move it by a day or a week.

Each move shows a message with **Undo**.

## Add a task on a day

1. Click an empty spot on any day.
2. Type the name in **What needs to get done?**
3. Press `Enter`. The task is due that day.

## Tasks with no dates

Tasks without dates wait in **No dates**. Drag them onto a day to give them a date, or drop a task on **No dates** to clear its dates.

## Options

Open **Calendar options** in the toolbar.

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Color by** | **Status** or **Priority** |
| **Show tasks on** | **Start to due** (a bar across every day) or **Due date only** |
| **Show weekends** | Shows or hides Saturday and Sunday |
| **Show subtasks** | Shows subtasks on the calendar |
| **Hide done tasks** | Hides finished tasks |

Save the layout as a named view with **Saved views**. See [views, filters and saved views](/docs/projects/views-and-filters).
