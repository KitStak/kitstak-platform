The **Workload** tab shows each person on a row and each week in a column. Each box shows how much open work that person has that week, compared with how much they can do. Red means too much. Drag a task to another person or week to even things out.

## Before you start

- To move tasks or change capacity you need the Editor or Owner role on the project.
- To count in points or hours, give tasks an estimate. See [work with tasks](/docs/projects/tasks).

## Pick how work is counted

1. Open the **Workload** tab.
2. Under **Measure work in**, pick **Tasks**, **Points** or **Hours**.

| Measure | What one task counts as |
| --- | --- |
| **Tasks** | 1 |
| **Points** | Its story points |
| **Hours** | Its time estimate, in hours |

Tasks with no estimate count as zero points or hours, and the row shows how many have **No estimate**. A task that runs across more than one week is split across those weeks, so each week counts its share.

## Set capacity

1. Select **Capacity**.
2. Under **Everyone, by default**, enter how much one person can take on in a week.
3. Optional: under **Different for someone?**, set a different number for a person.

Weeks above a person's capacity turn red. Until you set one, capacity is 8 tasks, 13 points or 40 hours a week.

## Balance the work

- Drag a task to another person's row to reassign it.
- Drag a task to another week to move its dates.
- Drop it on **No date** to clear its dates.

Each move shows a message with **Undo**. Select a person to show or hide their tasks, or use **Show everyone’s tasks** and **Hide everyone’s tasks**.

## Options

Open **Workload options**.

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Weeks to show** | **4 weeks**, **6 weeks**, **8 weeks** or **12 weeks** |
| **Hide people with no open work** | Hides rows with nothing to do |
| **Show builds** | In programs, counts builds too |

Use **Earlier weeks** and **Later weeks** to move through time, and **This week** to come back. Save the layout with **Saved views**.
