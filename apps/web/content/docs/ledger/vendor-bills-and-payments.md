> **Note:** Ledger is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

A bill is what a vendor sends you. Ledger is planned to hold every bill you owe, from the box supplier to the landlord, and track it from arrival to paid.

## Who sends bills

- Suppliers of boxes, film and labels.
- Temp agencies.
- Freight carriers.
- Rent and utilities.

## From bill to paid

1. A bill comes in. You add it, with the vendor, the amount, the due date and a copy of the bill.
2. Someone with the right permission approves it.
3. You schedule the payment for a date.
4. You record the payment once it is made.

## Match a bill to what came in

When a bill is for goods, Ledger is planned to match it to what was received, so you only pay for what arrived.

## Check a temp agency bill

If you use Crew, Ledger is planned to compare each agency bill with the hours Crew approved for that agency and week. When they differ, you see it before you pay: agency billed 412 hours, Crew recorded 396.

## Bills and job cost

A bill tagged to a job or a client is planned to count as a cost in Pulse, at the job, client or company level. See [How Ledger connects to the other apps](/docs/ledger/connected-apps).

## Vendors in one place

Temp agencies, suppliers and carriers are often the same companies across apps. Whether vendors become one shared list for Ledger, Crew and WMS is still being decided.
