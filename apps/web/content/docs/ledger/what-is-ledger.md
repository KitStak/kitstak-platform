> **Note:** Ledger is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

KitStak Ledger is the planned money app for kitting, 3PL and contract packaging shops. It turns your work into invoices, follows up until you are paid, pays your vendors, tracks expenses, matches it all to the bank, and closes the month.

The other KitStak apps already know what you earned and what the work cost. WMS builds bill lines and Pulse knows which work is not billed yet. Today someone retypes those into an accounting tool. Ledger is planned to close that loop.

## Billing and invoicing are not the same

| Word | What it means | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billing | What you pay KitStak for your plan. | The Portal. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started). |
| Invoicing | What your customers pay you for your work. | Ledger. |

Ledger never shows your KitStak plan, and the Portal's billing pages never show your customers' invoices. The two are kept apart on purpose.

## The words Ledger uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice | What you send a customer for work done. |
| Bill | What a vendor sends you, like a box supplier or a temp agency. |
| Expense | Card spend or a reimbursement, with a receipt. |
| Aging | How long each unpaid invoice has been open. |
| Reconciliation | Matching payments and deposits in the bank to invoices and bills. |
| Close | Locking a month so its numbers stop moving. |
| Accrual | Work done and not billed yet, booked at month end. |

## What Ledger is planned to cover

- [Invoices](/docs/ledger/invoices): drafted from WMS bill lines, Pulse work not billed yet, Projects price cards, or by hand.
- [Collections and aging](/docs/ledger/collections-and-aging): reminders, promises to pay, disputes and write offs.
- [Vendor bills and payments](/docs/ledger/vendor-bills-and-payments): approve, schedule and record what you owe.
- [Expenses](/docs/ledger/expenses): card spend and reimbursements with receipt photos.
- [Bank reconciliation](/docs/ledger/bank-reconciliation): match the bank to invoices and bills.
- [Month-end close](/docs/ledger/month-end-close): one close shared with Pulse.
- [How Ledger connects to the other apps](/docs/ledger/connected-apps): WMS, Pulse, CRM, Projects and Crew.

## Your accounting tool

Most shops already run QuickBooks or Xero. How Ledger works with them is still being decided:

- Ledger could send its entries to QuickBooks Online or Xero, so your accountant keeps working there.
- Or Ledger could hold your full books itself.
- Or it could start by sending entries, and hold the full books later.

Which accounting tool Ledger connects to first is also still being decided.

## What Ledger will not do at first

- Run payroll. Crew sends approved hours to your payroll provider, and Ledger books what comes back.
- File taxes. Ledger produces the reports, like sales tax collected and vendor totals. Filing stays with your accountant.
- Value inventory. Stock your 3PL clients own is not your asset. Supplies you buy count as an expense.

## Who sees money

Money is sensitive. Ledger is planned to use your KitStak roles with extra permissions to read and to change money, so a floor lead in WMS never sees what you owe.
