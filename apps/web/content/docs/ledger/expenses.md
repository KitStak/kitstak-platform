> **Note:** Ledger is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

Not everything arrives as a bill. Someone buys tape on the company card, or pays for a pallet jack part and wants the money back. Ledger is planned to hold those as expenses.

## Two kinds of expense

| Kind | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Card spend | A charge on a company card. |
| Reimbursement | Money a person spent and is paid back. |

## What an expense holds

- The amount and the date.
- A photo of the receipt.
- A category, like supplies, freight or repairs.
- A job or client, when the spend was for one.

## Expenses and job cost

An expense tagged to a job or client is planned to count as a cost in Pulse, so a rush shipment paid on a card shows up in that job's profit. An expense with no job or client counts at the company level.

## Supplies are an expense

Supplies you buy, like boxes and film, are planned to count as an expense or a small supplies account. Ledger does not keep a running value of stock. Stock your 3PL clients own is theirs, not yours.
