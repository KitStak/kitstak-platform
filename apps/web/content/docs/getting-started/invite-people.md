Invite your team once and they can use every app on your plan with one sign in. Owners and admins send invites from **Organization settings**. Each invite goes out by email with an **Accept invite** button.

## Before you start

- You are an owner or an admin. Other people see **Only owners and admins can invite members or change roles.** and who to ask.
- Your email is verified. Until it is, the form says **Verify your email first. It takes one minute. Then you can invite people.** See [Verify your email](/docs/getting-started/verify-your-email).

## Send an invite

1. Go to **Organization settings** and find **Invite someone** under **Members**.
2. Type an address in **Email**. To invite several people, put a comma between the addresses. You can invite up to 20 people at a time.
3. Pick a **Role**. See [Roles and what each can do](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
4. Read the line under the form. It says what the invite does to your seats.
5. Select **Send invite**.
6. The hint changes to **Invite sent to** the address. If the mail is slow, it says the invite **will go out in about a minute**.

The invite then shows under **Pending invitations** with the date it expires.

### Roles you can invite with

| Role | Uses a seat |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Yes |
| **Member** | Yes |
| **Viewer** | Yes |
| **Guest** | No. Guests see only what they are added to. |

To invite someone who only handles billing, use **Billing > Team** instead. See [Billing team and billing details](/docs/getting-started/billing-team-and-details).

### The seat line

| Your plan | What the line says |
| --- | --- |
| Free trial | **No seat limit during your free trial. Seats are counted when you pick a plan.** |
| Paid plan with open seats | **Uses** the number **of your open seats. Your bill stays the same.** |
| Paid plan, all seats used, you can pay | **We show the price and ask before we add** the seats. |
| Paid plan, all seats used, you cannot pay | **An owner or a billing manager can add one.** |
| Guest role | **A guest is free. This uses no seat.** |

## When every seat is in use

On a paid plan, an invite never raises your bill by itself.

1. When you send an invite with no seat free, the form stops and shows how many seats are in use.
2. An owner or billing manager sees the price of the extra seat and what is due today, then **Add seat and send invite** and **Not now**.
3. Select **Add seat and send invite**. The seat is added to the plan, then the invite goes out.
4. If the card is declined, nothing is charged and no invite is sent. Select **Update card**, then **Back to your invite** on the Billing page.

An admin who cannot pay sees who to ask. With Pulse alone, the plan covers up to 5 people and the form offers **Talk to us** instead.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Enter a valid email address. | Check the address. |
| Check this address: and an address | One address in the list is not valid. |
| Invite up to 20 people at a time. | Split the list. |
| A name **is already on your team.** | That person is already a member. |
| An address **already has an invite. It is in the list below.** | Look under **Pending invitations**. |

## How your teammate accepts

Your teammate gets an email from KitStak and selects **Accept invite**. The page reads **Join** your organization **on KitStak** and says who invited them and with what role.

| Their situation | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| New to KitStak | Fill in **Full name** (the **Work email** is fixed) and select **Join** your organization. No password is needed. **Set a password** opens a password field for those who want one. |
| Already has an account | Select **Sign in to accept**, then **Accept invite**. |
| Signed in with another email | The page says the invite is for another address. Select **Sign out and open the invite**. |
| Opened a copied link, not the one in the email | Select **Email me a code**, then enter the code sent to the invited address. |
| The team has no free seat | The page says the organization **has no free seat right now**. Nothing is created and nothing is charged. |
| The invite expired or was used | **This invite is no longer active.** Ask the sender for a new one. |

After joining, the organization becomes their current one and they land on the launcher. Invited people never see the app picker or a price.

## Manage pending invites

Under **Pending invitations**, each invite shows its address, its role and the day it expires, with **Pending** or **Expired**. Owners and admins can select **Cancel** to withdraw an invite. An expired invite no longer works, so send a new one.

An open invite holds a seat on a paid plan until it is accepted or canceled.
