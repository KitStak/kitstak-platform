The billing team is the people who can pay bills and change the plan. Owners are always on it. An owner can add any member as a billing manager, or invite someone, like a bookkeeper, for billing only. Billing details are the company, email, address and tax ID that print on your invoices.

## Who can manage billing

| Person | Can do in billing |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, and add or remove billing managers. Owners always manage billing. |
| Billing manager | Pay bills and update the card. Change the plan, seats and warehouses. Download invoices and edit billing details. |
| Admin | See billing, invoices, details and activity. Start the free trial and change trial apps. Cannot pay. |
| Any other member | A short card that names who manages billing. |

A billing manager gets **No extra access to your data.** The role covers billing only.

## Add a billing manager

You must be an owner. Others see **Only an owner can add or remove a billing manager.** and who to ask.

1. Open **Account menu > Billing** and select the **Team** tab. The page reads **The people who can pay and change the plan.**
2. Under **Add a billing manager**, pick a person from **Pick a person**. The hint says **Pick anyone on your team.**
3. Select **Add**.

The person now shows under **People who can manage billing** as **Billing manager**, with **Billing manager since** the day they were added.

> **Tip:** With only one person on the billing team, the page says **Add a second person so billing never gets stuck.** A second person means a bill can still be paid when the first one is away.

## Invite someone for billing only

Use this for a bookkeeper or anyone who pays the bills but does not use the apps.

1. On **Billing > Team**, find **Invite someone for billing only**. The hint says **Billing only. Free. No access to your apps. Good for a bookkeeper.**
2. Type their email address.
3. Select **Send invite**. You see **We sent an invite to** the address.

Until they accept, they show as **Invited** and **Billing only**, with **Cancel invite**. A billing only person uses no seat and has no access to CRM, WMS, Projects or Pulse. Their Portal home is Billing.

## Remove a billing manager

1. On **Billing > Team**, find the person.
2. Select **Remove**.

Owners cannot be removed from the billing team.

## Edit billing details

1. Open **Account menu > Billing** and select the **Details** tab. The page reads **These show on your invoices. Changes apply from your next invoice.**
2. Fill in the fields below.
3. Select **Save details**. Billing shows **Your billing details are saved.**

Only owners and billing managers can edit. Admins can read the page.

| Group | Field | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who we bill** | **Company name** | Shown on your invoices. Leave it empty to use your organization name. |
| **Who we bill** | **Billing email** | **Receipts go here, and to owners and billing managers.** |
| **Billing address** | **Address line 1**, **Address line 2**, **City**, **State or region**, **ZIP or postal code**, **Country** | Printed under **Billed to**. |
| **Tax ID** | **Type** | **Only if your business has one.** Pick **None** if not. |
| **Tax ID** | **Number** | Shown on your invoices. The hint shows the format for the type you picked. |

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Enter a valid billing email. | Check the address. |
| Pick the tax ID type. | Pick a type, or **None**. |
| Enter the tax ID number, or set the type to None. | Type the number, or set **Type** to **None**. |
