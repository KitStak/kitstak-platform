You can cancel your plan at any time in two clicks, with no call or email. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then your apps become view only. Until that day, one click undoes it. A monthly plan can pause instead. Only owners and billing managers can cancel or pause.

## Cancel your plan

1. Open **Account menu > Billing**.
2. On the Plan card, select **Cancel plan**.
3. The page **Cancel your plan** says what will happen, for example **Your plan will end on** a date. **You keep everything until then. After that your apps are view only. Nothing is deleted.**
4. Optionally answer **Why are you leaving?** See below. **You can skip this. It helps us get better.**
5. Select **Cancel my plan**. To stop, select **Keep my plan**.
6. Billing shows **Your plan is set to end. You keep everything until then. You can undo this any time before that day.**

The Plan card now reads **Your plan ends on** the date, with the badge **Ending**. You get an email confirming the end date.

### Why are you leaving?

Pick one, or none: **Too expensive**, **Missing something I need**, **Switching to another tool**, **Not using it enough**, **Our work is seasonal** or **Something else**. You can add a note in the text box. Skipping it never blocks the cancel.

### Remove one app instead

If your plan has two or more apps, the cancel page offers **Remove an app** to keep the rest. It opens the plan picker. See [Change your plan, seats and warehouses](/docs/getting-started/change-your-plan).

## Undo a cancel

Before the end date, open Billing and select **Keep my plan**. There is no extra confirm. You see **Your plan stays on. Nothing changes.**

## What cancel does in each case

| Your plan | What the cancel page says | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active and paid up | The plan ends on the last day you paid for. Yearly plans also say **You are paid up until** a date. | **Cancel my plan** |
| In the free trial with a card | **Your free trial still runs until** its last day. After that your apps are view only. Nothing is charged. | **Cancel my plan** |
| Paused | The plan ends and does not start again. | **Cancel my plan** |
| A bill is open and unpaid | **If you cancel now, your plan ends today** and the open bill is not charged. | **End my plan today**, or **Pay the bill instead** |
| Free trial with no card | **There is nothing to cancel**. The trial ends by itself and nothing is charged. | None |

> **Note:** When you cancel, your plan stays on until the end of the month or year you paid for. KitStak does not refund the time left. If you were charged by mistake, tell us through the [contact page](/contact).

Credit you have stays on your account. If you come back, it comes off your first bill.

## After the plan ends

- Your apps are view only. You can still open, read and export.
- Billing shows **Ended** and why the plan ended, with **Your data is safe.**
- Select **Pick a plan** to start again. There is no second free trial.

## Pause a monthly plan

A monthly plan in good standing can pause for 1, 2 or 3 months instead of canceling.

1. Open **Cancel plan** from Billing.
2. Under **Need a break?**, read **Pause for 1, 2 or 3 months. No bills while paused. Your plan starts again by itself.**
3. Select **1 month**, **2 months** or **3 months**. The page shows **Paused from** a date **Starts again on** a date.
4. Select **Pause my plan**. Billing says **Your pause is set. No bills while paused.**

The pause starts at the end of the period you already paid for. During the pause your apps are view only and nothing is deleted. Yearly plans cannot be paused.

### Undo or end a pause

| You want to | Do this | You see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undo a pause before it starts | Select **Undo pause** on Billing. | **Your pause is off. Your plan carries on as before.** |
| Start again early | Select **Start again now** on Billing. The card says what is billed today. | **Your plan is back on. Your apps are open again.** |
| Stop for good while paused | Select **Cancel plan**. | The plan ends and does not start again. |
