> **Note:** Crew and Ledger are planned and not available yet. Details may change before launch.

KitStak has two more apps planned. You cannot sign up for either one yet. See [pricing](/pricing) for what each is planned to cost. Their docs describe how each is planned to work, and will be rewritten against the real screens when each app ships.

## Crew: the people app

Crew is planned to keep a record of everyone on the floor, temps included, build the weekly schedule, run a time clock at the door, track attendance and skills, and tell you how many people tomorrow needs. Approved hours flow into Pulse as paid hours.

Read the planned docs: [KitStak Crew](/docs/crew). See the overview: [Crew](/crew).

## Ledger: the money app

Ledger is planned to turn WMS bill lines and Pulse work into invoices, follow up until you are paid, pay your vendors, track expenses, match the bank and close the month once for every app.

Ledger is for invoicing your own customers. Paying KitStak for your plan stays in the Portal under billing.

Read the planned docs: [KitStak Ledger](/docs/ledger). See the overview: [Ledger](/ledger).
