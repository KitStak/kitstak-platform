On a paid plan you can change anything at any time: add or remove an app, add or remove seats or warehouses, or switch between monthly and yearly. One rule covers every change. It starts right away, you pay only for the days left, and you get credit for what you give back. Nothing is charged until you confirm the amount.

## The one rule for every change

- The change starts right away.
- If it costs more, you pay now for the days left in the current period.
- If it costs less, the unused part becomes credit on your account. Credit comes off your next bills. It is not paid out as money.
- A day that has started counts as used. So adding a seat and removing it on the same day costs one day.
- The rule is the same for monthly and yearly plans.
- Nothing raises your bill by itself. A new invite or a new warehouse never adds a seat or a warehouse without someone saying yes first.

## Change your plan

1. Open **Account menu > Billing** and select **Change plan**, or open the **Plan** tab. The page is titled **Change your plan**: **Changes start right away. You pay only for the days left, and you see the amount first.**
2. Change apps, **People on your team**, **Warehouses** or **How often you pay**.
3. The summary card **Your new plan** shows **Now:** and **New:** with each total.
4. Select **Review change**. If you changed nothing, the button is off with **Change something to review it.**
5. A panel opens titled with what will change, such as **Add 1 seat**. It says **Check the amounts. Nothing is charged until you confirm.**
6. Check what is due today, any **Credit**, and the new price going forward.
7. Select **Confirm change**, or **Go back** to keep editing.
8. On success you land on Billing with **Your plan is updated.**

## Add an app

- On Billing, under **Your apps**, select **Add** on the app's row. The picker opens with that app turned on.
- Or, inside any app, select the app marked **Not in your plan** in the app switcher, then **Add** the app **to my plan** on its lock screen.

The price shown includes any bundle discount the new app brings. For numbers, see [Pricing](/pricing).

## Remove an app

Switch the app off in the picker and confirm. The unused part becomes credit. You cannot switch every app off. Then the hint says **To stop paying for everything, cancel your plan.** with a link to the cancel page.

## Seats and warehouses

| You want to | How | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add seats | Raise **People on your team**. | Up to 500. |
| Remove seats | Lower **People on your team**. | Not below the people you have plus open invites. |
| Add warehouses | Raise **Warehouses**. | Up to 50. |
| Remove warehouses | Lower **Warehouses**. | Not below the warehouses you have in the WMS. |

Billing shows your usage. If you pay for more than you use, the **Usage** section says how many you pay for and use, and what removing them would save.

### When you hit a limit

| Where | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Inviting in the Portal with every seat used | Owners and billing managers see the price and **Add seat and send invite**. See [Invite people](/docs/getting-started/invite-people). |
| Inviting from another app with every seat used | The app says the seats are in use and links to the plan picker. |
| Creating a warehouse in the WMS past your plan | The WMS shows the price and links to the picker with the warehouse count raised. |
| A member who cannot pay | The screen names who can add a seat. |

During a free trial there are no seat or warehouse limits.

## Monthly and yearly

Switch **How often you pay** and confirm. The new period starts at once. When you move from monthly to yearly, the unused part of the month comes off what you pay today. Yearly plans cannot be paused.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| The amount changed while you were looking. Check the new amount and confirm again. | Someone else changed the plan. Check the new amount. |
| Your plan already has this change. Nothing was charged. | The same change already went through. |
| That card was declined. Try another card or call your bank. | Nothing changed. Use **Update card**. |
| Another billing change is still running. Nothing was charged. Wait a moment and try again. | Wait and try again. |
| You use more than that today. Pick a bigger number, or remove people or warehouses first. | Raise the number. |

If someone else changed the plan while you looked, the panel keeps their change and only applies yours, so a second confirm never undoes theirs.
