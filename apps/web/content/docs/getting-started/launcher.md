The Portal home page is the launcher. It is titled **Your apps** and shows one tile for each app your organization can open, with a search box. Open an app from here and you are already signed in to it.

## Open an app

1. Sign in to the Portal. You land on **Your apps**.
2. Select an app tile. The whole tile is the link.
3. The app opens in a new tab.

| Tile | Line under the name |
| --- | --- |
| **KitStak CRM** | Deals and pipelines |
| **KitStak WMS** | Warehouse operations |
| **KitStak Projects** | Projects, programs and tasks, or a count of your projects and programs once you have some |
| **KitStak Pulse** | Job performance and profitability |

Only apps in your trial or plan, or added for you by KitStak, have a tile. Apps that are not in your plan are listed in Billing instead. See [App access](/docs/getting-started/app-access).

## Search your apps

Type in **Search apps…** to filter the tiles. If nothing matches, the page says so.

## The plan note on a tile

A tile can carry one quiet line about the plan. An app that is on and paid for says nothing.

| Note | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| The days left in your free trial | The app is in your trial. |
| **Free trial. First payment on** a date | You added a card during the trial. |
| **Payment past due. Open until** a date | A payment failed. The app stays fully open until that date. |
| **View only.** and the fix | The app opens, but you cannot change anything. Owners and billing managers see what to do. Others see who to ask. |

A view only tile still opens the app, so you can read and export your data.

## When no app shows

If your organization has no app yet, the page says **No apps on your plan yet**.

| Who you are | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Owner, admin or billing manager | **Add CRM, WMS, Projects or Pulse to start using them here.** with **Start free trial** or **See plans** |
| Anyone else | **Ask** the owner **to add CRM, WMS, Projects or Pulse.** with **Ask for an app** |

## When you are in no organization

If you were removed from your organization, or you left it, the launcher reads **You are no longer in an organization**. Your account still works. Ask a teammate to invite you again, or start your own: type a name in **Company or team** and select **Create organization**.

## The Portal top bar

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| KitStak logo | Back to **Your apps**. |
| Organization switcher | Shows your organization and lets you switch. See [Organizations](/docs/getting-started/organizations). |
| Gear | Opens **Organization settings**. |
| Your initials | Opens the **Account menu**: **Apps**, **Billing**, **Organization settings**, **My account**, **Light or dark theme** and **Sign out**. |

> **Tip:** Press Ctrl and K, or the slash key, to open the command palette. Type an app, a page or an organization name and press Enter.
