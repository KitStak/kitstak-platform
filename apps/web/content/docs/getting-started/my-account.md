**My account** holds your own details: your name, your email and how you sign in. One account works in every KitStak app, so a change here shows everywhere. Your organization and your role are separate, in **Organization settings**.

## Open My account

1. In the Portal top bar, select your initials to open the **Account menu**.
2. Select **My account**.

The page reads **Your name, your email and how you sign in. One account works in every KitStak app.**

## The cards on the page

| Card | What it shows | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | **Your full name**, with the hint **Shown to your team in every KitStak app.** | Edit it and select **Save name**. The hint changes to **Saved.** |
| **Email** | Your address with **Verified** or **Not verified**. | If not verified, select **Verify my email**. |
| **Password** | Whether you have a password. | **Change my password**, or set one with **Set a password**. |
| **Ways to sign in** | Each way in and whether it is set. | Read only. |

## Change your name

1. In the **Name** card, edit **Your full name**.
2. Select **Save name**. The button is off until the name changes.

Your new name shows to your team in every KitStak app.

## Verify your email

If the **Email** card says **Not verified**, it also says **Verify your email with a 6 digit code. Then you can invite people and pay.**

1. Select **Verify my email**.
2. Enter the code. See [Verify your email](/docs/getting-started/verify-your-email).

Once verified, the card says **You sign in with this email. Receipts and invites use it too.**

## Set a password

If you have no password, the **Password** card says you sign in with a 6 digit code, or with Google or Microsoft if you use them.

1. Type a password in **New password**. The meter and checklist show under it. See [Password rules](/docs/getting-started/password-rules).
2. Select **Set a password**.
3. You see **Your password is saved. You can sign in with it, or with a code, from now on.**

If you see **To keep your account safe, sign in again first. Then set your password.**, select **Sign in again**, then come back and set it.

## Change your password

1. In the **Password** card, select **Change my password**.
2. Follow [Reset or change your password](/docs/getting-started/reset-your-password).

## Ways to sign in

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **A code by email.** | Always there. Pick **Email me a code instead** on the sign in page. |
| **A password.** | **Set.** or **Not set.** |
| **Google.** or **Microsoft.** | Shown with **Connected.** when you signed in that way. |

## When your old password was removed

If you see a notice that starts **You are signed in. Your old password no longer works**, your email was not verified before you first signed in with a code. KitStak removed the old password to keep your account safe. Set a new one in the **Password** card, or keep signing in with a code.
