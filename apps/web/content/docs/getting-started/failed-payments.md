If a renewal charge fails, your apps stay fully open for a grace period while KitStak tries the card again. Owners and billing managers are told by email and by a banner in every app. If the bill is still not paid when the grace period ends, the apps become view only until it is. Nothing is deleted, and paying turns full access back on at once.

## What happens, step by step

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| The charge fails | The plan shows **Payment due**. Owners and billing managers get an email and see a banner in every app. Full access continues. |
| Each retry that fails | A new email. The banner and Billing show the next try date. |
| Your bank asks you to approve | An email asks you to approve the payment. |
| Near the end of the grace period | An email titled **Last day to fix your KitStak payment**. |
| The grace period ends unpaid | The apps become view only. The badge reads **View only**. |
| You update the card or select **Pay now** | The open bill is paid at once and full access returns. You get a receipt. |
| The last try fails | The plan ends. The Plan card says how many tries failed and when the plan ended. Apps stay view only. **Pick a plan** starts a new plan. |

The exact dates are always on screen. Billing shows the next try date and the date your access changes, so you never have to count days.

## Where you see it

| Place | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Billing, Plan card | **We could not charge your** card **for** the amount, and **We will try again on** a date. |
| Billing, Next charge card | **We will try** the amount **again. Your apps stay open until** a date. |
| Portal home tiles | **Payment past due. Open until** a date. |
| App switcher | The badge **Payment due**. |
| A banner in each app | The amount, the card, the next try and the date to fix it by, with **Update card**. |

Admins read the same facts with the name of the person to ask. Other members see no banner until the apps become view only.

## Fix a failed payment

1. Open **Account menu > Billing**, or select **Update card** in the banner or the email. The Billing page opens with the payment card in focus.
2. Select **Update card**. A secure page opens to change the card. Card payments run on Stripe. KitStak never sees your full card number.
3. Save the new card. You come back to Billing, and KitStak tries the open bill at once.
4. You see **Card updated. Your payment went through.** Your apps are fully open again.

If the card is updated but the bill did not go through yet, you see **Card updated, but the payment did not go through yet. Try Pay now.**

### Pay with the card on file

If the card on file should work now, select **Pay now** on the Plan card. On success you see **Your payment went through. Your apps are fully open.**

## While the apps are view only

- Everyone can still open, read, search and export.
- Changes are refused with **Your plan is view only right now. Pick a plan in the Portal to make changes.**
- The Plan card says **Your payment is late, so your apps are view only.**
- Nothing is deleted.

## Cancel with a bill open

If you cancel while a bill is unpaid, the plan ends today and that bill is dropped. The cancel page says so before you confirm, with the button **End my plan today**, and offers **Pay the bill instead**. See [Cancel or pause your plan](/docs/getting-started/cancel-or-pause).
