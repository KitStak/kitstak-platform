Right after sign up, owners and admins see a short welcome screen. It shows your apps, asks at most three questions, and builds a setup plan from your answers. The plan then lives in the setup guide in **Organization settings**, where steps check themselves off as you finish them.

## Before you start

- You are an owner or an admin. Other roles go straight to the Portal home page.
- You have started a free trial or have apps on your plan. See [Create your KitStak account](/docs/getting-started/sign-up).

## Go through the welcome screen

The welcome screen has four steps. A bar at the top shows where you are. **Skip for now** is always at the top.

1. **Your apps are ready.** Your apps are listed first, each marked **In your free trial** or **Ready to use**. Apps you do not have are marked **Not added yet**. To change the mix, select **Change your apps**. Select **Continue**.
2. **What kind of business are you?** Pick one tile, then pick **How big is your team?**. Select **Continue**.
3. **How do you want to start?** Pick one tile. Select **Continue**.
4. **Here is your setup plan.** Check the steps and the minutes each takes. Select **Looks good**.

You go to the setup guide in **Organization settings**. Use **Back** to change an answer before you finish.

### The questions and choices

| Question | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **What kind of business are you?** | **3PL warehouse**, **Brand or online store**, **Distributor or wholesale**, **Something else** |
| **How big is your team?** | **Just me**, **2 to 10**, **11 to 50**, **More than 50** |
| **How do you want to start?** | **Start fresh**, **Explore sample data**, **Import my data** |

Your answers shape the plan:

- **Just me** leaves out **Invite your team**. It comes back if a teammate or an invite shows up.
- The kind of business sets the order of the steps. A 3PL or a distributor gets warehouse steps first. A brand gets CRM steps first.
- **Import my data** points the catalog step at the WMS imports. **Explore sample data** points the CRM step at ready made templates.

> **Tip:** If you answered these questions on the KitStak website, the welcome screen opens on the last step with your answers filled in. Select **Change answers** to edit them, or **Looks good** to go on. Nobody answers the same question twice.

### Skip the welcome screen

Select **Skip for now** at any point. You go to the Portal home page. Answers you already gave count as done. If you gave none, **Tell us about your business** stays open in the setup guide.

## Use the setup guide

1. Go to **Organization settings**. Select the gear in the Portal top bar, or open **Account menu > Organization settings**.
2. Find the card titled **Set up** and your organization name, like **Set up Bluefin Packaging**.
3. The line under the title shows how many steps are done and about how many minutes are left.
4. The next step has the filled button. Select it to go straight to the right screen in the right app.
5. When you finish the work, the step checks itself off. There is nothing to tick by hand.

You can also open the guide from the command palette: press Ctrl and K, then pick **Setup guide**.

### Step status

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A number | The step is still to do. |
| **Done** | KitStak found the work in the app, for example a deal on a CRM board. |
| A crossed out title with **Undo skip** | Someone skipped it. Select **Undo skip** to bring it back. |
| **Skip** | Skips this step. Owners and admins can skip. |

The guide shows a short list first. The rest sit behind **Show 3 more steps for later** (the number changes), and **Hide the steps for later** folds them away again. Steps for later do not change the count.

### The steps

Only the steps for your apps appear. **Pick your plan** is always last.

| Step | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| **Tell us about your business** | Three quick questions to shape this plan. |
| **Invite your team** | Teammates sign in once and get every app your plan includes. |
| **Create your CRM workspace** | Pick a ready made template: boards for deals, contacts and accounts. |
| **Add your first deal** | Put one real deal on the board. |
| **Create your warehouse** | Name your facility and lay out zones and locations. |
| **Build your product catalog** | Create items one by one, or import your whole catalog from a spreadsheet. |
| **Create your first order** | Run one order through pick, pack and ship. |
| **Start your first project** | Pick a template, or start from a blank board. |
| **Give someone a task** | Assign one task with a due date. |
| **Connect work to a deal or order** | Link a project or task to a CRM deal or WMS order. |
| **Set your labor cost per hour** | What an hour of work really costs you. |
| **Add your first job** | A job is one piece of work for one client, with one price. |
| **Log a day of work** | Type the units made, the people and the hours. |
| **Link a job to a deal or work order** | A linked job fills in its own client, quantity and price. |
| **Pick your plan** | Shown during the free trial to owners and billing managers. Done once a card is on file or the plan is paid. |

When every step is done, the guide says **Setup complete**. If some were skipped, it says **Setup wrapped up**.

> **Note:** Guests and people who only manage billing do not see the setup guide.
