Your organization keeps all rights to the content it stores in KitStak. Nothing is deleted when a free trial ends, a payment is late, a plan is paused or a plan is canceled. You can export your data at any time, even when your apps are view only, and an owner can ask KitStak to delete the account.

## What stays when you stop paying

| What happened | Your data | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The free trial ended with no plan | Kept | Open, read and export. Pick a plan to work again. |
| A payment is late past the grace period | Kept | Open, read and export. Pay to work again. |
| The plan is paused | Kept | Open, read and export. It starts again by itself. |
| The plan was canceled and ended | Kept | Open, read and export. Pick a plan to come back. |

View only never blocks exports or downloads. See [App access](/docs/getting-started/app-access).

## Export your data

| Data | Where to export it |
| --- | --- |
| CRM, WMS, Projects and Pulse records | Each app has its own exports. See the [CRM docs](/docs/crm), [WMS docs](/docs/wms), [Projects docs](/docs/projects) and [Pulse docs](/docs/pulse). |
| Invoices | **Billing > Invoices**, the PDF link on each invoice. |
| Billing activity | **Billing > Activity**, then **Download CSV**. |

The billing activity file has these columns:

```
When (UTC),Kind,Who,What happened
```

## Remove a person's access

1. Go to **Organization settings** and find the person under **Members**.
2. Select **Remove** and confirm. They lose access to every app at once.

A person can also leave by themselves with **Leave organization** in **Danger zone**. See [Organizations](/docs/getting-started/organizations).

## Delete your organization

Organization owners can ask KitStak to delete the whole account.

1. Export anything you want to keep first.
2. Cancel your plan. An organization with a live plan cannot be deleted. The refusal says **Cancel your plan before you delete this organization.** See [Cancel or pause your plan](/docs/getting-started/cancel-or-pause).
3. Ask for deletion through the [contact page](/contact). Write from the owner's email and name the organization.

## Your personal data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information. You can change your own name in **My account**. To ask for more, contact your organization administrator, or see the [Privacy policy](/privacy) for how to reach KitStak. The [Terms](/terms) describe your rights to your content.
