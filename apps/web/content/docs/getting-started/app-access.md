For each app, your organization has one of three levels of access. Full access lets you do everything. View only lets you open, read and export, but not change anything. Not in your plan means your organization has not added the app yet. Every app and the Portal use the same rules and the same words.

## The three levels

| Level | You can | Happens when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full access | Do everything your role allows. | The app is in a running free trial or a paid plan, or a payment failed and is still inside the grace period. |
| View only | Open, read, search and export. No changes. | The free trial ended with no plan, a payment is late past the grace period, the plan is paused, or the plan ended. |
| Not in your plan | See a lock screen with a way to add the app. | Your organization never added the app. |

> **Important:** Nothing is ever deleted by a trial ending, a late payment, a pause or a cancel. Your data waits for you, and paying turns full access back on at once.

## View only

When an app is view only:

- A banner at the top of the app says why and has one button to fix it, such as **Pick a plan**, **Update card** or **Start again now**.
- Before a form opens, a small dialog titled **Your plan is view only right now** explains, with the same button.
- If a change still reaches the server, it is refused with **Your plan is view only right now. Pick a plan in the Portal to make changes.**
- Exports and downloads keep working.

Only owners and billing managers see a pay button. Admins read the same facts and who to ask. Other members see a banner only when the app is view only, for example **This app is view only right now.** with the name of the person to ask.

## Not in your plan

Opening an app your organization has not added shows a lock screen in the app.

| Who you are and your plan | Title | Main button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can start a trial, no trial used yet | The app **is not in your plan yet** | **Start free trial** |
| In a free trial | The app **is not in your trial yet** | **Add** the app **to my trial** |
| Owner or billing manager on a paid plan | The app **is not in your plan yet** | **Add** the app **to my plan** |
| Admin on a paid plan | The app **is not in your plan yet**, with who to ask | None |
| Any other member | The app **is not in your plan yet**, with who to ask | A button that asks the owner to add it |

Every lock screen also has **Back to Portal**. The lock screen shows the price the app would add. For the numbers, see [Pricing](/pricing).

## Add an app from inside another app

1. Open the app switcher and select the app marked **Not in your plan**.
2. The Portal opens the plan picker with that app already switched on.
3. During a free trial, select **Save my apps**. The new app shares the trial end date.
4. On a paid plan, select **Review change**, check the amount, and select **Confirm change**.
5. On the Billing page, a **Back to** button returns you to the app you came from.

## Apps KitStak added for you

An app marked **Included** was added by KitStak for your organization. It shows **Free for your organization. It is never billed.** Trials, checkout and plan changes never change or remove it.
