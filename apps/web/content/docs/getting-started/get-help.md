## Talk to us

1. Go to the [contact page](/contact).
2. Under **What can we help with?**, pick a topic. For a bill or an invoice, pick **Billing help**. For anything that does not fit, pick **Something else**.
3. Fill in **Full name**, **Work email** and **Company**, then write your question.
4. Press the button at the bottom. It opens your email app with the message addressed to KitStak. Nothing is sent from the page itself.

Include your organization name and what you were trying to do. Never send a password or a full card number.

## A question about a charge or an invoice

Every billing page in the Portal ends with the line **A question about a charge, an invoice or your plan?** It shows the KitStak billing email address, written out so you can copy it, and a **talk to us** link to the website.

1. Open **Account menu > Billing**.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Email the address shown, or select **talk to us**.

Before you write, check [Invoices and billing activity](/docs/getting-started/invoices-and-activity). It lists every payment, plan change and billing email with the date and who did it.

## Ask a teammate

Some answers belong to your own team. The screens name the person to ask.

| You see | Who can help |
| --- | --- |
| **Only owners and admins can invite members or change roles.** | An owner or admin, named on the same line. |
| **Only an owner or a billing manager can change the plan** | The owner named on the screen. |
| A button like **Ask Jordan to add Projects** on the Billing page | The owner named on the button gets your request by email. |
| **This invite is no longer active** | The person who invited you can send a new one. |
