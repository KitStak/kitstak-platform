The free trial gives your organization full access to the apps you pick, with no card. There is one trial for the whole organization and one end date for every app in it. When it ends with no plan, your apps become view only and nothing is deleted. For the trial length and prices, see [Pricing](/pricing).

## Rules of the free trial

- No card is needed to start, and nothing is charged during the trial.
- You get full access to every app in the trial.
- Apps you add during the trial share the same end date.
- There is no seat limit and no warehouse limit during the trial.
- Each organization gets one free trial, and so does each person who starts one. A person who already started a trial for another organization can still pick a plan, but gets no second trial.
- Owners, admins and billing managers can start the trial and change its apps.

## Start the free trial

Most people start it at the end of sign up. See [Create your KitStak account](/docs/getting-started/sign-up). If your organization has no plan yet:

1. Open **Account menu > Billing**. The Plan card says **You have no plan yet.**
2. Select **Start free trial**.
3. On **Pick your apps**, switch on the apps you want.
4. Select **Start my free trial**. You see **Your free trial has started. No card needed.**

You can also start it from the lock screen of an app that is not in your plan: select **Start free trial**.

## Add or remove trial apps

1. Open **Account menu > Billing**.
2. On the Plan card, select **Change trial apps**. The page is titled **Change your trial apps**: **Add or remove apps any time. Apps you add share the same trial end date.**
3. Switch apps on or off.
4. Select **Save my apps**. You see **Your trial apps are saved.**

If nothing changed, the button is off with the hint **These are the apps in your trial now.**

To add one app from inside another app, select it in the app switcher, or select **Add** the app **to my trial** on its lock screen.

## Where you see the trial

| Place | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Billing, Plan card | **Free trial.** with the days left and the end date |
| Billing, Next charge card | **No charge planned** and **Pick a plan to keep your apps after** the end date |
| Portal home tiles | The days left in your free trial |
| A banner in each app | The end date, with **Pick a plan** for owners and billing managers. It does not show on the first day. Near the end it turns to a warning, and on the last day it says **Your free trial ends today.** |
| Email | A welcome email when it starts, and reminders before it ends while no card is on file |

## Add a card during the trial

Adding a card early never costs you trial days.

1. On Billing, select **Pick a plan**, choose your plan and pay. See [Pick a plan and pay](/docs/getting-started/pick-a-plan).
2. The success page says **Your card is saved. Your free trial carries on, and nothing is charged today.**
3. The Plan card now reads **Free trial. Your first payment is on** the day the trial ends.
4. The setup guide step **Pick your plan** is done.

On that day the first charge runs and your plan becomes active with no break in access.

## When the trial ends

| You added a card | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Yes | The first payment runs and the plan becomes active. You get a receipt by email. |
| No | Your apps become view only. The Plan card says **Your free trial ended** with the date, **Your data is safe.**, and **Pick a plan**. You get the email **Your KitStak trial has ended. Your data is safe.** |

While view only, everyone can still open, read and export. When an owner or billing manager picks a plan, full access comes back at once. You can pick a plan on any later day.

## Cancel during the trial

You do not need to cancel a trial with no card. It ends by itself and nothing is charged. If you added a card and change your mind, use **Cancel plan**. The cancel page says your free trial still runs until its last day, then your apps are view only. See [Cancel or pause your plan](/docs/getting-started/cancel-or-pause).
