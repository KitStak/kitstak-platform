Signing up makes three things at once: your account, your organization, and your free trial. It takes two short steps, and no card is needed. If you were invited by a teammate, do not sign up here. Open the link in your invite email instead (see [Invite people](/docs/getting-started/invite-people)).

## Before you start

- Use your work email. Receipts, invites and sign in codes go to it.
- Have your company or team name ready. You can change it later.

## Step 1: your account

1. Open the sign up page from the KitStak website, or select **Start your free trial** next to **New to KitStak?** at the bottom of the sign in page.
2. The page is titled **Start your free trial** and the step reads **Your account**.
3. To use a Google or Microsoft account, select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft** when those buttons are shown. You then name your organization on the next screen.
4. Or fill in the four fields below the line **or use your email**.
5. Select **Create account**. The button reads **Creating your account…** and then **Setting up your organization…** while it works.

| Field | What to enter | Rule the form checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Full name** | Your first and last name, like Jordan Lee. | 2 to 100 characters. |
| **Work email** | The address you use for work. | Must look like a real address. A likely typo, like gmial.com, is offered back to fix in one press. |
| **Password** | A password only you know. | See [Password rules](/docs/getting-started/password-rules). The checklist under the field ticks off each rule as you type. |
| **Company or team** | The name of your organization, like Bluefin Packaging. | 2 to 80 characters. |

Each field is checked when you leave it and again when you press the button. A problem shows under its field and the cursor moves to the first one. Nothing you typed is cleared.

> **Tip:** Name, email and company are kept in this browser tab until your account exists, so a refresh does not empty the form. The password is never stored.

### Sign up with no password

1. Under **Create account**, select **Email me a code instead**.
2. The password field is replaced by **No password needed**. KitStak emails you a 6 digit code each time you sign in.
3. To go back, select **Set a password instead**.

You can set a password later in [My account](/docs/getting-started/my-account).

### Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Enter your full name. | Type at least 2 characters. |
| Enter a valid work email. | Check the address for typos. |
| Enter your company or team name. | Type at least 2 characters. |
| An account with this email already exists. Sign in to keep going. | Select **Sign in**. Your email is filled in for you. |
| We could not reach KitStak. Check your connection and try again. | Check your internet and press the button again. |
| Your account is ready, but we could not set up your organization. Try again. | The form keeps only **Company or team**. Select **Continue**. |

## Step 2: pick your apps

After step 1 you land on **Pick your apps**, marked **Your apps**. If you came from the KitStak website with apps already chosen, they are switched on for you.

1. Switch on each app you want to try. Tap anywhere on its row.
2. If CRM, Projects or Pulse is on, set **People on your team**. If WMS is on, set **Warehouses**.
3. Under **How often you pay**, choose **Pay monthly** or the yearly option. This is only what the plan will be after the trial.
4. Check the summary card **Your free trial**. It shows each app, the price **After your trial**, **Due today** and the day the trial ends.
5. Select **Start my free trial**. If no app is on, the button is off and says **Pick at least one app.**

The card under the button says **No charge today.**, **Cancel any time in Billing.** and **Your data stays yours.**

> **Note:** A person who already used a free trial with another organization sees **You already used your free trial. You can still pick a plan.** The button then goes to payment instead.

For prices and discounts, see [Pricing](/pricing).

## What happens next

1. If your email is not verified yet, you see **Check your email**. See [Verify your email](/docs/getting-started/verify-your-email).
2. Then the welcome screen asks a few questions and builds your setup plan. See [Welcome screen and setup guide](/docs/getting-started/welcome-and-setup-guide).
3. You are the owner of the new organization. See [Roles and what each can do](/docs/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).

## Name your organization later

If you signed up with Google or Microsoft, or the organization step failed, you see **Name your organization**.

1. Type the name in **Company or team**. The hint says **You can change this later.**
2. Select **Continue**.
3. You go on to **Pick your apps**.

The bottom of the screen shows **Signed in as** your email, with **Sign out** if this is not your account.

## Pick up where you left off

You can close the tab at any point. When you come back and sign in, KitStak opens the step you were on.

| Where you stopped | Where you land |
| --- | --- |
| Signed in, no organization yet | **Name your organization** |
| Organization made, no trial or plan yet | **Pick your apps** |
| Trial started, email not verified and not skipped | **Check your email** |
| Welcome questions not answered | The welcome screen |
| Everything done | The Portal home page |
