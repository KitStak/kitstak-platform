An organization is your company or team in KitStak. Its members, their roles, its apps and its plan all belong to it. You make one when you sign up, and you can belong to more than one, for example your own company and a client who invited you.

## Open Organization settings

1. In the Portal top bar, select the gear, or open **Account menu > Organization settings**.
2. The page is titled **Organization**.

| Part of the page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | **Organization name** and **Slug**. |
| Setup guide | Your setup steps. See [Welcome screen and setup guide](/docs/getting-started/welcome-and-setup-guide). |
| **Members** | Everyone in the organization, with their role. |
| **Invite someone** | The invite form, for owners and admins. |
| **Pending invitations** | Invites not accepted yet, each **Pending** or **Expired**. |
| **About** | **Created**, **Members** and **Your role**. |
| **Danger zone** | **Leave organization**. |

## Rename your organization

1. In **Profile**, edit **Organization name**. It must be 2 to 80 characters.
2. Select **Save changes**. The hint shows **Saved.**

Only owners and admins can edit the profile. Others see **Read-only. Only owners and admins can edit.**

The **Slug** is read only. It **Identifies this organization across every KitStak app. Slugs can’t be changed here.**

## Switch organizations

1. In the Portal top bar, select the organization switcher. It shows your current organization name.
2. Under **Organizations**, select the one you want. A check marks the current one.
3. The Portal reloads for that organization. Its apps, people and billing are now the ones you see.

You can also press Ctrl and K and pick an organization under **Switch organization**.

## Links for one organization

A link from a billing email or from a banner in another app can name its organization. When you open it:

- If you are a member, that organization becomes the current one and the page opens.
- If you are signed out, you sign in first and then go to the page.
- If you are not a member, you see **This link is for another organization.** with **Go to my Portal**. Sign in with the account that got the email, or ask the owner to invite you.

## Leave an organization

1. In **Danger zone**, select **Leave organization**.
2. The page says you will lose access to the organization across every KitStak app.
3. Select **Yes, leave** and the organization name to confirm, or **Cancel**.

A member with invite rights can bring you back with a new invite.

> **Note:** If you are the only owner, you cannot leave. The page says **You’re the only owner** and asks you to promote another member to owner first.

## Start another organization

If you are in no organization, the launcher reads **You are no longer in an organization** and shows a form. Type a name in **Company or team** and select **Create organization**. You become its owner.
