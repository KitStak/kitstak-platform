Every KitStak app has the same app switcher in its top bar. It lists the Portal and the four apps, with a badge for each one's plan state. You sign in once at the Portal, and every app opens with that same account. There is no second sign in.

## Switch apps

1. In any app, select the grid button **Switch app** in the top bar.
2. The menu **KitStak apps** opens. The app you are in is marked **Current app**.
3. Select another app. A short screen reads **Taking you there with the same account** while the next app opens.

To open an app in a new tab, hold Ctrl (or Cmd on a Mac) while you select it.

| Row | Line under the name |
| --- | --- |
| **KitStak Portal** | Launcher, billing and organization |
| **KitStak CRM** | Pipelines, boards and automations |
| **KitStak WMS** | Receiving, inventory and shipping |
| **KitStak Projects** | Projects, programs and tasks |
| **KitStak Pulse** | Profit by job, client and service |

## Badges in the switcher

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Trial** | The app is in your free trial. |
| **Active** | The app is on your paid plan. |
| **Payment due** | A payment failed. The app is still open while it is fixed. |
| **View only** | You can open and read the app, but not change anything. |
| **Not in your plan** | Your organization has not added the app. Selecting it opens the plan picker with the app turned on. |
| **Included** | KitStak added the app for your organization. It is never billed. |

> **Note:** A person who only manages billing has no app access. Their app rows read **Billing only. Ask an owner for access.**, and the Portal row opens Billing.

## Switch from the Portal

The Portal has no app switcher in its own top bar. Use the tiles on **Your apps**, or the command palette:

1. Press Ctrl and K (or the slash key).
2. Under **Apps**, pick **Open CRM**, **Open WMS** and so on. An app that is not in your plan reads **Locked. View billing** and opens Billing.
3. Under **Portal**, pick **Apps**, **Setup guide**, **Billing** or **Organization settings**.
4. Under **Switch organization**, pick another organization you belong to.

## How one sign in works

- You sign in only at the Portal. Each app checks the same session.
- If you open a link to an app while signed out, the app sends you to the Portal sign in page. After you sign in, you go back to the page you asked for.
- The Portal only sends you back to KitStak addresses, never to another site.
- Your light or dark theme follows you from app to app.
- A link that names an organization opens in that organization, if you belong to it. See [Organizations](/docs/getting-started/organizations).
