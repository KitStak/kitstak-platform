All billing for every KitStak app lives in one place: **Billing** in the Portal. It answers what you are on, what it costs, when the next charge is, which card pays, and where your invoices are. The page always shows one main button for the next step.

## Open Billing

1. In the Portal top bar, select your initials to open the **Account menu**.
2. Select **Billing**.

Owners, admins and billing managers see the full page with these tabs: **Overview**, **Plan**, **Invoices**, **Details**, **Team** and **Activity**. Other members see one card instead. See **Who sees what** below.

## The four cards

| Card | What it shows | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Your apps, seats and warehouses, monthly or yearly, and one line about where the plan stands. | The main button, such as **Pick a plan** or **Change plan** |
| **Cost** | What the plan costs each month or year, and whether the bundle discount is in it. | **See the math**, which opens **How your price adds up** |
| **Next charge** | The date and what the card will really be charged, less any promo code or credit. | None |
| **Payment method** | The card brand, its last four digits and when it expires, or **No card yet**. | **Update card** when a card is on file |

## Below the cards

- **Usage**: seats and warehouses in use against what you pay for, with a hint when you pay for more than you use.
- **Your apps**: every app with its badge. An app not on your plan has an **Add** button and the price it would add.
- **Invoices**: your newest invoices with a PDF link.
- **Billing details**: company, billing email and tax ID, with **Edit**.
- **Billing team**: who can manage billing, with **Manage**. With only one person it says **Add a second person so billing never gets stuck.**

## What the Plan card says

| Badge | Line on the Plan card | Main button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No plan** | **You have no plan yet.** and a line to try or pick apps | **Start free trial** or **Pick a plan** |
| **Free trial** | **Free trial.** with the days left and the end date | **Pick a plan** (also **Change trial apps**) |
| **Free trial** | **Free trial. Your first payment is on** a date | **Change plan** |
| **Trial ended** | **Your free trial ended** on a date. Your apps are view only. **Your data is safe.** | **Pick a plan** |
| **Active** | **Your plan is active.** | **Change plan** (also **Update card**, **Cancel plan**) |
| **Payment due** | **We could not charge your** card, with the amount and the next try | **Update card** (also **Pay now**, **Cancel plan**) |
| **View only** | **Your payment is late, so your apps are view only.** | **Update card** (also **Pay now**, **Cancel plan**) |
| **Ending** | **Your plan ends on** a date. **You keep everything until then.** | **Keep my plan** |
| **Pause planned** | **Your plan pauses on** a date **and starts again on** a date. | **Undo pause** |
| **Paused** | **Your plan is paused. It starts again on** a date. | **Start again now** (also **Cancel plan**) |
| **Ended** | Why and when the plan ended. **Your data is safe.** | **Pick a plan** |

## Messages after an action

| Message | After you |
| --- | --- |
| Your free trial has started. No card needed. | Started a trial |
| Your trial apps are saved. | Changed trial apps |
| Your plan is updated. | Confirmed a plan change |
| Your plan is set to end. You keep everything until then. You can undo this any time before that day. | Canceled |
| Your plan stays on. Nothing changes. | Selected **Keep my plan** |
| Your pause is set. No bills while paused. | Paused |
| Your pause is off. Your plan carries on as before. | Selected **Undo pause** |
| Your plan is back on. Your apps are open again. | Started again after a pause |
| Your payment went through. Your apps are fully open. | Paid an open bill |
| Your billing details are saved. | Edited billing details |

## Who sees what

| Who | What they see |
| --- | --- |
| Owner or billing manager | Everything, and every button. |
| Admin | Everything. Where a pay or change button would be, the card says **Only an owner or a billing manager can pick a plan and pay.** or **Only an owner or a billing manager can change the plan.** An admin can still start the free trial and change trial apps. |
| Any other member | One card: who manages billing, the plan state in one line, and **Back to my apps**. Under it, **Apps you do not have yet** lists apps to ask for. |

### Ask for an app

1. As a member, open **Account menu > Billing**.
2. Under **Apps you do not have yet**, select the button that asks the owner to add the app, like **Ask Jordan to add Projects**.
3. KitStak emails your ask. The button changes to **Asked today**. You can ask once a day for each app.

## When billing is off

If billing cannot run, a notice says **Billing is not available right now. Nothing will be charged. Please try again later.** Money buttons are off. Reading still works and you can still start a free trial.
