KitStak is a set of four business apps that share one account. You sign in once, invite your team once, and pay one bill. The Portal is the home base: it holds your apps, your people and your billing.

This page explains the parts and where each job is done. Start here if you are new.

## The four apps and the Portal

| App | What it is for | Docs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KitStak CRM | Track every customer, deal and follow up in one place. | [CRM docs](/docs/crm) |
| KitStak WMS | Run receiving, inventory, picking and shipping in your warehouse. | [WMS docs](/docs/wms) |
| KitStak Projects | Plan work, assign tasks and see what is due. | [Projects docs](/docs/projects) |
| KitStak Pulse | See the profit on every job, client and month. | [Pulse docs](/docs/pulse) |
| KitStak Portal | Launcher, billing and organization. | This guide |

You can use one app or all four. Each app works on its own. When you have more than one, records can link across them, so a won deal can start a project and a Pulse job can show its profit on the deal. See [How records link across apps](/docs/getting-started/linking-records).

## What you share across every app

- **One account.** Your name, email and sign in work in every app. See [Manage your account](/docs/getting-started/my-account).
- **One organization.** Your company or team in KitStak. People, roles and the plan belong to it. See [Organizations](/docs/getting-started/organizations).
- **One sign in.** Sign in at the Portal and open any app with no second sign in. See [Switch between apps](/docs/getting-started/switch-apps).
- **One free trial.** Pick the apps you want and try them with no card. See [How the free trial works](/docs/getting-started/free-trial).
- **One bill.** Every app you pay for is on one plan and one invoice. See [Billing overview](/docs/getting-started/billing-overview).

## Where each job is done

| You want to | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Open an app | The Portal home page, **Your apps** |
| Invite a teammate or change a role | **Organization settings** in the Portal |
| See your setup steps | **Organization settings**, the setup guide |
| Change your name or set a password | **Account menu > My account** |
| Pick a plan, add an app, change seats | **Account menu > Billing** |
| Download an invoice | **Billing > Invoices** |
| Do the daily work | The app itself: CRM, WMS, Projects or Pulse |

## Words you will see

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Organization | Your company or team in KitStak. You can belong to more than one. |
| Owner | The person who creates an organization is its owner. Owners can do everything. |
| Seat | One person on your team who can sign in. Guests are free. |
| Warehouse | One building you run in the WMS. |
| Plan | The apps you pay for, with how many seats and warehouses. |
| Free trial | Full access to the apps you pick, with no card. |
| View only | You can open, read and export everything, but not change it. |
| Not in your plan | An app your organization has not added yet. |

## Get going

1. [Create your account](/docs/getting-started/sign-up) and pick your apps.
2. [Verify your email](/docs/getting-started/verify-your-email) so you can invite people and pay.
3. Answer the [welcome questions](/docs/getting-started/welcome-and-setup-guide) to get a short setup plan.
4. [Invite your team](/docs/getting-started/invite-people).
5. [Open your apps](/docs/getting-started/launcher) and start work.

For prices, see [Pricing](/pricing).
