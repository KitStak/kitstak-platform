Picking a plan is how you keep your apps after the free trial, or start paying without one. It is two steps: the plan picker, then a secure pay page where you enter your card. You always see what is due today and the first charge before you pay. Only owners and billing managers can pay.

## Before you start

- You are an owner or a billing manager. Admins see **Only an owner or a billing manager can change the plan** and who to ask.
- Your email is verified. If not, the pay button is off with **Verify your email first. It takes one minute.** and an **Enter code** link.
- For prices, discounts and how they add up, see [Pricing](/pricing).

## Pick your plan

1. Open **Account menu > Billing** and select **Pick a plan**. The page is titled **Pick your plan**: **Choose your apps, seats and how often you pay. You enter your card on the next screen.**
2. Under **Apps**, switch on each app you want. With one app on, a note says a second app saves on both.
3. Set the numbers that apply to your apps.
4. Under **How often you pay**, pick **Pay monthly** or the yearly option.
5. Check the summary card **Your plan**. See the next section.
6. Select **Continue to secure checkout**.

| Setting | Shown when | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **People on your team** | CRM, Projects or Pulse is on | **One seat for each person who signs in. Guests are free.** It cannot go below the people you have plus open invites. |
| **Warehouses** | WMS is on | **One for each building you run in the WMS.** It cannot go below the warehouses you have in the WMS. |
| **How often you pay** | Always | Monthly or yearly. Yearly costs less per month. |

When a number cannot go lower, the hint says why, for example **You have 6 people. Remove someone first to go lower.**

### Seats for each app

- CRM and Projects are priced for each seat, and they share one seat count.
- WMS is priced for each warehouse.
- Pulse has one flat price for the whole team. Alone it covers up to 5 people. With CRM or Projects in the plan, it covers every seat for the same flat price.

If Pulse is your only app and you have more than 5 people, the picker says so and offers **talk to us**.

## The summary card

| Line | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| One line for each app | The math for that app: count, price and total. |
| **List price** | The total before discounts. |
| **Yearly discount** | Shown when you pay yearly. |
| **Bundle discount** | Shown when you have two or more apps. |
| **After your trial** or the total | What you pay each month or year. |
| **Due today** | What the card is charged now. During a free trial this is zero. |
| **First charge** or **Next charge** | The amount and the date of the first charge on the card. |

Under it: **No charge today.** or the amount you pay today, then **Cancel any time in Billing.** and **Your data stays yours.**

> **Tip:** A promo code is entered on the next screen, the secure pay page.

## Pay on the secure page

1. After **Continue to secure checkout**, you go to the secure pay page. Card payments run on Stripe. KitStak never sees your full card number.
2. Enter your card, and a promo code if you have one.
3. Pay. You come back to the Portal.

| What you see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **You are all set.** with **Your card is saved. Your free trial carries on, and nothing is charged today.** | You paid during the trial. The first charge is on the trial end date. |
| **You are all set.** with **Your payment went through. Your apps are fully open.** | You paid after the trial or with no trial. |
| **Finishing up.** and **This takes a few seconds.** | The payment is being confirmed. The page checks again by itself. |
| **No charge was made. Your cart is saved.** | You left the pay page. Select **Go back to checkout** or **Change my plan**. |

The success page shows your plan, the first or next charge, your card, and says where the receipt or plan details were emailed. Select **Open my apps** to go back to work.

## The review page

Some links open **Review your plan** first: **Check the plan and the dates. You enter your card on the next screen.** It shows the same lines, **Due today** and **First charge** or **Next charge**. Select **Continue to secure checkout**, or **Change my plan** to go back to the picker.

## Messages you may see

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Pick at least one app. | Switch on an app. |
| You use more than that today. Pick a bigger number, or remove people or warehouses first. | Raise the number, or remove people or warehouses first. |
| That card was declined. Try another card or call your bank. | Use another card. |
| Your bank wants you to approve this payment. Nothing was charged yet. Use another card, or call your bank. | Approve it with your bank, or use another card. |
| Billing is not available right now. Nothing will be charged. Please try again later. | Try again later. |
