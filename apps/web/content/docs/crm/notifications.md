Notifications tell you when something needs your attention: someone mentions you, assigns you, replies to you or shares a board with you. They arrive in the bell in the top bar as they happen, without a page refresh. You never get a notification for your own actions.

## What sends a notification

| Label | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Mention** | Someone @mentions you in an update or a reply |
| **Assignment** | Someone adds you to a people column, by hand, by import or by automation |
| **Reply** | Someone replies to your update |
| **Update** | Someone posts an update on an item where you are in a people column |
| **Reaction** | Someone reacts to your update |
| **Board shared** | Someone adds you to a board |
| **Invitation** | You are invited |
| **Automation** | A board automation’s **Notify** action names you or a people column you are in |

## Open the bell

Select the bell in the top bar. The badge shows how many notifications are unread, for example **Notifications (3 unread)**.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every notification |
| **Unread** | Only unread ones, with a count |
| **Mentions** | Only mentions |
| **Assigned** | Only assignments |

Search with **Search notifications** and narrow to one board with the board filter. Notifications are grouped under **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Earlier**. Several similar notifications are folded together: select **+2 similar** to open them and **Hide similar** to fold them again.

Select a notification to open the item or board. Press `Esc` or select **Close notifications** to close the panel.

## Manage one notification

Hover a notification and open **More actions**.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open item** or **Open board** | Opens where it happened |
| **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread** | Changes its read state |
| **Turn off notifications for this item** | Stops update notifications for the item. A toast offers **Undo** |
| **Delete notification** | Removes the notification |

Turning off an item’s notifications removes you from its subscribers. Mentions, assignments and invitations still reach you.

## Panel options

Open **Notification options** in the panel header.

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark all as read** | Marks every notification read. With a filter on, it reads **Mark these as read** |
| **Open inbox** | Opens the Inbox page |
| **Notification settings** | Opens the **Notifications** tab of **My profile** |

## Item subscribers

The subscriber list in the item panel shows who follows an item: the people in its people columns, its creator, people who posted updates on it and anyone added by hand. To stop update notifications for an item, open it and select **Unsubscribe me** under the subscriber avatars, or use **Turn off notifications for this item** in the bell. See [Open an item: updates, files and activity](/docs/crm/item-panel).
