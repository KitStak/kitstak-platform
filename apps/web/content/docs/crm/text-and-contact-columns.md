These columns hold the words and contact details of an item: a lead’s company and title, a contact’s email and phone, an account’s website and head office. Most of them validate what you type and let you copy the value in one click.

## Text

A short line of free text, such as a job title or a next step. Select the cell, type and press `Enter`. Text columns can be sorted and filtered.

## Long text

Multi-line notes. Select the cell (**Add notes**) to open the editor. Select **Expand editor** for more room and **Collapse editor** to go back. Long text can be filtered but not sorted.

## Email

1. Select the cell (**Add email**).
2. Enter the **Email**, for example `jordan.lee@example.com`.
3. Optionally enter **Text to display**, for example `Jordan Lee`.
4. Save.

The address must be valid. Otherwise you see **Enter a valid email, like name@company.com**. Use the copy button (**Copy email**) to copy the address.

## Phone

1. Select the cell (**Add phone**).
2. Pick the country from the flag menu. Search with **Search country or code**.
3. Enter the **Phone number**, for example `214 555 0163`.
4. Save.

Use digits, spaces, `()`, `.` or `-`. Otherwise you see **Use digits, spaces, (), . or -**.

## Link

1. Select the cell (**Add link**).
2. Enter the **Web address**, for example `www.example.com`.
3. Optionally enter **Text to display**, for example `Pricing page`.
4. Save.

The address needs a valid host name. Addresses without `http://` or `https://` get `https://` added. Anything else shows **Enter a web address starting with http:// or https://**.

## Location

1. Select the cell (**Add location**).
2. Type in **Search an address or place**.
3. Pick a suggestion under **Places** or **Used on this board**, or press `Enter` to keep what you typed.

A typed address without a match is saved with **No coordinates (typed address)**.

## Country

Select the cell (**Pick a country**) and choose from the list. The cell shows the country with its flag. Search with **Search countries**.

## Copy and paste

In the table you can copy a cell with `Ctrl C` and paste it into another cell with `Ctrl V` (`Cmd` on a Mac).
