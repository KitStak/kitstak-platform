My work collects every item where you are in a People column, on every board you can open, into one list. It groups them by date so you can see what is late, what is due today and what comes next.

## Open My work

Select **My work** in the top bar, or press `G` then `M`. Home shows a short version under **My work due this week**.

## How the list is grouped

| Group | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Past dates** | Items whose date has passed |
| **Today** | Items due today |
| **This week** | Items due later this week |
| **Next week** | Items due next week |
| **Later** | Items due after next week |
| **Without a date** | Assigned items with no date |
| **Other assigned items** | Contacts, accounts and boards without a due date |

Each row shows the **Item**, its **Board**, its **Status** and its **Date**. Select the item to open it.

**Other assigned items** can be long. Select **Hide them** to leave them out, and **Show them** to bring them back.

## Update items from the list

- Select the status to pick another label.
- Select the date to change it. Quick picks are **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next Monday** and **In 2 weeks**. Empty dates show **Add date**.
- Select the done checkbox (**Mark done**) to set the item to its Done label. A toast offers **Undo**.

You need edit access to the item’s board to change it.

## Find items

- Search with **Search my items**.
- Filter by board with **All boards** or one board.
- Turn on **Show done** to include finished items. They appear under **Completed**.

## Empty states

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing assigned to you yet** | You are not in a People column on any item |
| **You're all done** | Nothing with a due date is assigned to you right now |
| **No items match** | Try a different search or board |
