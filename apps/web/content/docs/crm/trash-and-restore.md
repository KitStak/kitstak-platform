KitStak CRM gives you two ways to take something out of the way. Archive keeps it but out of sight or read-only. Delete moves it to the trash, where it stays for 30 days before it is removed for good.

## Archive or delete

| Action | Board | Group | Item |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Archive | The board stays in place and becomes read-only until someone selects **Restore board** | **Archive group** takes the group and its items off the board | **Archive** takes the item off the board |
| Delete | **Delete board…** moves the board and all its items to the trash | **Delete group** moves the group and its items to the trash | **Delete** moves the item to the trash |

Archiving or deleting items and groups shows a toast with **Undo**. Use it right away to put things back as they were.

## Open the trash

1. Select your avatar in the top bar.
2. Select **Trash**.

The **Trash** page lists deleted boards, groups and items with their **Name**, **Location**, when they were **Deleted** and when they will be **Removed in**. If nothing is there, it says **Trash is empty**.

## Find an entry

- Search with **Search the trash**.
- Filter by type: **All**, **Boards**, **Groups** or **Items**.
- Filter by board with **All boards** or a board name.
- Select **Clear filters** to start over.

You only see entries from boards you can access. Org admins see everything.

## Restore an entry

1. Select the entry, or several with their checkboxes.
2. Select **Restore**.

A toast confirms how many entries were restored. Restoring a group brings back exactly the items that were deleted with it. If you don’t have edit access to the board, the restore action is disabled with **You need edit access to this board to restore it**.

## Delete an entry forever

1. Select the entry.
2. Select **Delete forever** and confirm.

Deleting a board forever erases everything inside it: groups, items, updates and files. Deleting an item forever erases its updates, files and history. This can’t be undone.

> **Important:** Only board owners and admins can delete permanently.

## Rules

- Entries stay in the trash for 30 days, then they are removed permanently.
- Deleting a workspace moves its boards, dashboards and folders to the trash for 30 days.
- Deleting a folder does not delete its boards. They move up a level.
