Each board has its own members and privacy, on top of the organization roles. Use the share dialog to decide who can see a board, who can change it and who just follows it.

## Before you start

- You need edit access to the board to change its members or privacy.
- People must already belong to the organization. To bring in someone new, use **Invite to KitStak** in the dialog. See [Manage the organization and its users](/docs/crm/organization-and-users).

## Open the share dialog

Do one of these on the board:

- Select **Invite** in the board header.
- Select the member avatars in the header.
- Open **More board actions** and select **Permissions & privacy**.

The dialog title is **Share** followed by the board name.

## Add people to a board

1. In **Add people by name or email**, search for a teammate.
2. Pick their role in **Role for new member**.
3. Select **Add**. A toast confirms the person and role.
4. Select **Done**.

If everyone is already on the board, the search says **Everyone in your organization is already on this board.**

## Board roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Manage settings, members and structure |
| **Editor** | Edit items and columns |
| **Viewer** | View and comment only |
| **Subscriber** | Gets notifications |

To change a role, use the role menu on the person’s row. To remove someone, select **Remove** and their name. A board always keeps at least one owner.

## Board privacy

| Privacy | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| **Main** | Everyone in the workspace can see it |
| **Private** | Only board members can see it |
| **Shareable** | Members can invite guests from outside |

Select a privacy option in the dialog. A toast confirms, for example **Board is now private**. On a Main board with no members added, everyone in the workspace can still see it.

## How access is decided

- Organization owners and admins can see every board.
- Main boards in open workspaces are visible to all members who are not guests.
- Closed workspaces need a workspace membership first.
- Private and shareable boards need a board membership.
- Guests only see boards they were added to.
- Board members with the **Viewer** role can’t make changes.

## Copy the board link

Select **Copy link** in the dialog, or **Copy board link** in the board menu. The link only opens for people who have access.
