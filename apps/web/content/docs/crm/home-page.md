Home is the first page you see after you sign in. It brings together what you opened last, what is due this week and what changed on the items you follow. Select the KitStak logo or **Home** in the top bar to return to it, or press `G` then `H`.

## What Home shows

| Area | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting | The date, your organization and a greeting with your first name |
| Quick actions | **Invite**, **Import** and **New board**, each shown when your role allows it |
| **Getting started** | The setup guide checklist, until it is done or dismissed |
| **Recently visited** | Cards for the boards, dashboards and workspaces you opened last |
| **My work due this week** | Items assigned to you that are due this week, with overdue ones flagged |
| **Latest updates** | New updates on items you follow |
| **Inbox** | Your unread count and an **Open** link |
| **Favorites** | Starred boards, dashboards and folders |
| **Start from a template** | Shortcuts into the template center |

When nothing is due, **My work due this week** says **You're all caught up** and **Nothing assigned to you is due before the weekend.** Select **See all** to open My work.

## Work through the setup guide

The **Getting started** card lists four steps. Each one opens the place where you do it, and checks itself off when the work is done.

1. Select a step to open it: **Browse templates**, **Open your pipeline**, **Invite teammates** or **Create a dashboard**.
2. To leave a step for later, select **Skip**. The step shows **Skipped. You can finish this any time.** Select **Undo skip** to bring it back.
3. When every step is done, the card shows **All set**. Select **Dismiss** to hide it.

Steps that depend on the CRM workspace show **Locked** until that step is done. To hide the guide early, open the card menu (**Setup guide options**) and select **Dismiss guide**.

> **Note:** Only people who can manage organization settings can dismiss or restore the guide for everyone. To bring it back, open **Help > Setup guide** in the top bar.
