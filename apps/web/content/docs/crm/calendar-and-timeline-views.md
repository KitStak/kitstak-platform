Calendar and timeline views place items by their dates. Use the calendar to see what happens on which day, such as close dates or meetings. Use the timeline to plan work that spans days or months, such as onboarding projects.

## Before you start

- The board needs a **Date** or **Timeline** column. Without one, the calendar says **Add a date to see the calendar** and the timeline says **To use this view add a Timeline or Date column**.
- You need edit access to reschedule items or change the view’s settings.

## Use the calendar

1. Select **Add view** and pick **Calendar**.
2. Pick the **Calendar range**: **Month**, **Week** or **Day**.
3. Use the previous and next arrows to move, and **Today** to come back.
4. Drag an item to another day to change its date.
5. Select an empty spot on a day to quick-create an item on that date. Type the name and press `Enter`.

Select **Unscheduled** to open the tray of items without a date. Drag one onto a day to schedule it.

The Deals template has a **Close dates** calendar, and the Activities template has a **Calendar** of activity dates.

## Calendar settings

Open **Customize** (**Calendar settings**).

| Section | Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dates** | **Date column** | The date or timeline column to plot |
| **Dates** | **Week starts on** | **Sunday** or **Monday** |
| **Dates** | **Event length** | The length of timed events in week and day ranges: 30 min to 4 h |
| **Dates** | **Hide weekends** | Leaves Saturday and Sunday out |
| **Appearance** | **Color by** | A status or dropdown column, **Group** or **Single color** |
| **Appearance** | **Open unscheduled tray by default** | Shows the tray when the view opens |
| **Appearance** | **Single-key shortcuts** | Turns the keys below on or off for you on this device |

With **Single-key shortcuts** on and the calendar grid focused: `T` goes to today, `M`, `W` and `D` switch to month, week and day, `N` creates an item today, and the left and right arrows move the range.

## Use the timeline

1. Select **Add view** and pick **Timeline**.
2. Pick a **Zoom**: **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months** or **Quarters**.
3. Select **Auto fit** to fit every bar, or **Today** to jump to today.
4. Drag a bar to move its dates. Drag either end to resize it.

A bar with only one date shows as a milestone. With the keyboard, focus a bar and use the arrow keys to move it, or `Shift` and the arrow keys to resize it.

The Deals template has a **Deal timeline** view, and Customer onboarding has a **Gantt** view.

## Timeline settings

Open **Customize** (**Timeline settings**).

| Section | Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bars** | **Dates from** | The timeline or date column that draws the bars |
| **Bars** | **Color by** | A status or dropdown column, **Group** or **Single color** |
| **Rows** | **Group by** | **Group**, a status or dropdown column, or **No grouping** |
| **Rows** | **Group summary bars** | Shows one bar spanning each group’s items |
| **Rows** | **Today line** | Marks today on the timeline |
| **Rows** | **Shade weekends** | Shades Saturdays and Sundays |
| **Rows** | **Single-key shortcuts** | `T` for today and `Z` for auto fit while the timeline has focus |
