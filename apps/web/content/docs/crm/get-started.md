KitStak CRM keeps your sales and customer work on boards: leads, deals, accounts, contacts, activities and anything else your team tracks. This page walks you through the first sign-in and the setup flow, and explains what the CRM builds for you.

## Before you start

- You need a KitStak account with the CRM in your plan. New accounts are made in the KitStak portal. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).
- If a teammate invited you, open the join link they shared and sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to.

## Set up your organization

The first time you open the CRM without an organization, the setup flow starts with **Welcome. Let’s set up KitStak.** Each step has **Continue**.

1. Enter your **Company or team name**.
2. Under **What best describes your role?**, pick the closest match, such as **Account executive**, **Sales leader** or **Customer success**.
3. Answer **How big is your team?**: **Just me**, **A small, close team**, **A growing sales team**, **Several teams** or **Enterprise rollout**.
4. Under **What do you want to manage?**, pick everything that applies: **Leads**, **Deals & pipeline**, **Contacts**, **Accounts**, **Calls & meetings**, **Customer onboarding**, **Renewals** or **Quotes & proposals**.
5. Under **Pick your starter CRM**, choose a starter. The flow recommends one from your answers.
6. Leave **Include sample data** on if you want example deals and accounts to explore. You can clear them any time.
7. Under **Invite your teammates**, add email addresses, or skip the step. Select **Invite and finish** or **Finish setup**.
8. When you see **Your workspace is ready**, select **Go to my workspace**.

If setup could not finish, the flow says so and lets you try again. Your account is already created at that point.

## Starter CRMs

| Starter | Boards it creates |
| --- | --- |
| **Full sales CRM** | Leads, Deals, Accounts, Contacts and Activities, connected, plus a sales dashboard |
| **Pipeline essentials** | Deals with the Accounts and Contacts they belong to |
| **Prospecting** | Leads, Contacts and Activities |
| **Customer success** | Accounts, Contacts, Customer onboarding and Renewals |

The boards are linked with connect columns, so a deal points to its account and contacts, and the account shows its deals. See [The CRM boards](/docs/crm/crm-boards).

## What to do next

The Home page shows a **Getting started** card with four steps that check themselves off as you work:

| Step | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| **Create your CRM workspace** | Start from a template with leads, deals, contacts and accounts |
| **Add your first deal** | Add a deal to your pipeline |
| **Invite your team** | Give deals, activities and accounts owners |
| **Create a dashboard** | Pipeline value, win rate and team performance |

**Add your first deal** and **Create a dashboard** stay locked until the CRM workspace exists.

> **Tip:** You can reopen the checklist any time from the **Help** menu in the top bar with **Setup guide**.
