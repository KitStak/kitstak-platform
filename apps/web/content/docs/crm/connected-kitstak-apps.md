When your organization uses other KitStak apps, CRM records connect to them. An account can show its warehouse client, and deals and accounts can show the projects, programs and job profit that come after the sale. Each connection appears only when your plan includes that app.

## Before you start

- The other app must be in your organization’s plan. Use the app switcher in the top bar to open it. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started).
- Connections show on top-level records only, not on subitems.

## Link an account to a WMS client

The **Warehouse (WMS)** card on an account’s 360 page links the account to a client in [KitStak WMS](/docs/wms).

1. Open the account’s 360 page. See [Account and contact 360 pages](/docs/crm/account-and-contact-pages).
2. In **Warehouse (WMS)**, search in **Search WMS clients…** by name or client code.
3. Pick the client and select **Link**.

Once linked, the card shows the client’s name and status, its **Client code**, and its **Open orders** and **On hand** units. Select **Unlink** to remove the link. You need edit access to the Accounts board to link or unlink, and each organization sees only its own warehouse clients.

## Plan work in KitStak Projects

When your plan includes [KitStak Projects](/docs/projects), deals, accounts and other top-level records get a **Projects** tab in the item panel.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Create task** | Opens **Create a task in Projects**: **Task name**, **Project**, **Status**, **Priority**, **Assignee**, **Due date** and **Notes** |
| **Start a project** | Opens the Projects wizard, filled in from this record |
| **Link a project** | Connects a project you already have. Its progress shows here |
| **Open in Projects** | Opens a linked project in KitStak Projects |
| **Unlink from this record** | Removes the link. The project stays in KitStak Projects |

Tasks you create here land in KitStak Projects and stay linked to the record. Turn on **Hide done** to show only open tasks.

## Track programs

On deals and accounts, the **Programs** tab shows programs from KitStak Projects that track repeat builds under one price card. Each program shows what was built this month, a forecast for the month and today’s builds. A won deal with no program yet offers **Start a program**. Select **Open in Projects** to see a program in full.

## See job profit from KitStak Pulse

When your plan includes [KitStak Pulse](/docs/pulse) and you have a Pulse role, deals and accounts get a **Profit** tab.

- On a deal, **Job profit** shows each Pulse job made from the deal, with **Revenue so far**, **Cost so far**, **Profit so far** and **Margin**, and **Margin by month**. Select **Create Pulse job from this deal** if your Pulse role can add jobs.
- On an account, **Client profit** shows profit for the **Last 90 days** and **Last 12 months**, and the client’s **Open jobs**.

The numbers are the same ones you see in KitStak Pulse, read when the tab loads. Select **Open KitStak Pulse** to see every entry behind them.
