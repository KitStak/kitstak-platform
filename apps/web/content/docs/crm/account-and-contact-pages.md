Every account and contact has a 360 page. It brings together what you know about the relationship: the full activity history, what is planned next, the open and won deals, the people involved and every field from the board. You can log a call or schedule a follow-up without leaving the page.

## Before you start

- You need Accounts or Contacts boards from the CRM templates.
- To log activities, you need an Activities board.

## Open a 360 page

1. Open an account or contact on its board.
2. In the item panel header, select **Open 360 view**.

You can also open a 360 page from links in the pipeline, lead conversion and other 360 pages. The breadcrumb leads back to **Home**, **CRM** and the board. If a record was deleted, merged or lives on a board you can’t open, the page says **This account isn’t available** or **This contact isn’t available**, with **Back to pipeline**.

## The header

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Name and initials | The record’s name. Contacts also show their title and account |
| **Owner** | The record’s owners |
| **Open in board** | Opens the record in its board’s item panel |
| **Log activity** | Opens the activity composer on this page |
| **Send email** and **Call** | On contacts with an email or phone. Each has a copy button |
| **Open website** | On accounts with a website |

## The activity timeline

The **Activity** card shows the record’s history and what is planned, newest first, split into **Upcoming** and **History**. An account’s timeline includes the activity of its deals.

Filter it with **All**, **Upcoming**, **Calls**, **Emails**, **Meetings** or **Notes & updates**. With no activity yet, it says **No activity yet** and **Log the first call, email or meeting to start the history.**

## The side cards

| Card | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Next steps** | Upcoming activities and open next steps. Empty, it offers **Schedule follow-up** |
| **Deals** | The linked deals with their stage, and the **Open**, **Weighted** and **Won** totals |
| **Account** | On a contact: its account. If none is linked, link one from the board |
| **Related contacts** | On a contact: other contacts at the same account |
| **Contacts** | On an account: its contacts |
| **Warehouse (WMS)** | On an account: its linked KitStak WMS client. See [Link CRM records to other KitStak apps](/docs/crm/connected-kitstak-apps) |
| **Details** | Every field of the record |

## Log an activity

1. Select **Log activity** in the header. On a board, you can also select **Log activity** in the item panel of a contact, account or deal.
2. Pick the activity type, such as **Call**, **Meeting**, **Email**, **Demo** or **Task**. The types come from the Activities board.
3. Enter a **Subject**, a short summary.
4. Pick the **Outcome**: **Completed**, **Connected**, **Left voicemail**, **No answer**, **Scheduled (upcoming)** or **Cancelled**.
5. Set the **Date**, and a **Time** or **No time**. Enter the **Minutes** it took.
6. Write **Notes**: what happened, what was agreed, what’s next.
7. Under **Linked to**, check the contacts and deals it belongs to. At least one is needed.
8. Select **Log activity**, or **Schedule** for an upcoming one.

A toast confirms, for example **Call logged** or **Meeting scheduled**. The activity is added to the Activities board, linked to the contacts and deals you checked, and shows in their timelines.

## Schedule a follow-up

1. In **Next steps**, select **Schedule follow-up**. The composer opens with **Scheduled (upcoming)**.
2. Fill in the type, subject and date.
3. Select **Schedule**.

## Rules

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Activity type | Required. **Pick an activity type** if none is chosen |
| **Subject** | Up to 200 characters |
| **Minutes** | A whole number, up to one day |
| **Notes** | Up to 10,000 characters |
| **Linked to** | At least one contact or deal, up to 20 of each |

If saving fails, your notes stay in the composer so you can try again.
