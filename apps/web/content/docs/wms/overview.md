KitStak WMS runs a warehouse from the dock to the door. You set up warehouses, locations and products, receive stock against purchase orders, put it away, count it, and track every unit by location, license plate, lot, expiry date and serial number. This page explains how the app is organized and where to start.

## What you can do

- Keep a product catalog per client, with pack types, barcodes, kits and packaging material. See [Manage the product catalog](/docs/wms/products).
- Receive purchase orders on a desktop or a handheld, then put the stock away. See [Purchase orders](/docs/wms/purchase-orders).
- Build your storage layout one location at a time or generate a whole rack layout. See [Manage locations](/docs/wms/locations).
- See stock by SKU, by location and as available to promise. See [Total inventory and available to promise](/docs/wms/total-inventory-and-atp).
- Count stock with count plans, quick counts and recounts, and post the variance. See [Count plans](/docs/wms/count-plans).
- Follow and act on every open task. See [Task details](/docs/wms/task-details).

## How the app is organized

The top bar holds seven menus. Each one opens a panel with columns of pages. You only see the pages your role allows.

| Menu | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Fulfillment** | Shipment orders, packing, returns, **Task Management** (jobs and task details) and shipments |
| **Inventory** | **Inbound** (purchase orders, receiving history, work orders), **Inventory** (totals, by location, ATP, kits) and **Count Operations** |
| **Automation** | Algorithms, workflows, rate shopping and wave rules |
| **Integrations** | Sales channel and carrier connections, logs and monitoring |
| **Reports** | Order, inventory, log, receiving and efficiency reports |
| **Analytics** | Labor and operational dashboards, when those apps are turned on |
| **Settings** | **Master Data** (products, locations, vendors), **Configuration** (warehouse, LP types, LP numbers), **Client Setup**, **Company** and **Users** |

**Dashboard** sits to the left of the menus. See [Navigate the WMS](/docs/wms/navigate-the-app) for search, shortcuts and the header icons.

## Key ideas

- **Warehouse.** Every location, license plate, purchase order and count belongs to one warehouse.
- **Client.** Products, purchase orders and orders belong to a client, the company you store stock for. One warehouse can serve many clients.
- **Location.** A place that holds stock, such as a bin, a shelf or a receiving station. Each location has an area type, such as **Receiving**, **Storage** or **Picking**.
- **License plate (LP).** A container, such as a pallet or a tote, that holds stock and moves as one. See [License plates](/docs/wms/license-plates).
- **Inventory row.** Stock of one SKU in one location, with its LP, lot, expiry date, production date and damage reason.

## Where to start

1. Read [Set up your warehouse step by step](/docs/wms/setup-sequence) for the order to create things in.
2. Use the **Get set up** checklist on the dashboard. It checks off each step from live data. See [Use the dashboard](/docs/wms/dashboard).
3. Load data in bulk with Excel templates. See [Import data from Excel](/docs/wms/excel-imports).

> **Tip:** KitStak WMS runs in the browser on desktops and on handheld scanners. See [Receive on a handheld](/docs/wms/rf-receiving) and [Enter counts on the count sheet](/docs/wms/count-sheet).
