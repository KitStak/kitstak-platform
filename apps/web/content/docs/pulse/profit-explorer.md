The **Profit** page answers one question for every job, client and service: are we making money, and if not, why? Pick how to cut the numbers with the tabs, pick a period, and click any row to go one level down. The total of each level always matches the row you clicked.

## Before you start

- **Profit** needs a role that sees money. Supervisors do not see it. See [Roles and who sees money](/docs/pulse/roles-and-access).

## Pick the cut

Go to **Profit** and pick a tab:

| Tab | One row per |
| --- | --- |
| **Jobs** | Job. |
| **Clients** | Client. Select a client to open its page. See [Set up and review clients](/docs/pulse/clients). |
| **Job types** | Kind of work. |
| **Extra services** | Extra service. Work with no service rolls up under **Core job work**, so the total equals the company total. |
| **Sites** | Site, side by side: margin, pace and time not on a job. |
| **Programs** | KitStak Projects program. Shown when Projects is connected. |
| **Months**, **Weeks**, **Days** | Period. Closed months are locked so the numbers stay put. |

## Pick the period and filters

1. Pick a period: **This week**, **Last week**, **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This quarter**, **This year** or **Custom** (with a **First day** and **Last day**).
2. Under **Compare with**, pick **No comparison**, **The period before** or **Same period last year**. Change columns appear.
3. Filter by site, client, job type and job status.

The period and filters live in the page address, so you can bookmark or share any view.

## Pick how to show the numbers

| Mode | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Table** | The rows and columns below. |
| **Chart** | A ranking bar chart of profit or margin, **Best first** or **Worst first**. Time tabs show revenue and cost bars with the job margin line next to your target margin. |
| **Contribution** | Each row's share of profit. |
| **Pareto** | Rows ranked with a running share, so you see which few make most of the profit. |
| **Pivot** | One cut down the side and another across the top. |
| **Cost to serve** | What each row costs, per unit and per job. |

## The columns

Open **Columns to show** to add or hide columns. **Back to the usual columns** resets them.

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Revenue** | What the work earned. Work done, not invoices sent. |
| **Labor** | Labor hours times the real cost of an hour. |
| **Material** | Parts you paid for, scrap included. |
| **Other** | Other costs typed in against a job or a client. |
| **Time not on a job** | Paid time for meetings, cleanup and waiting, spread over the jobs. |
| **Cost** | Labor, material, other and time not on a job, added up. |
| **Profit** | Revenue less cost. |
| **Job margin** | Profit as a share of revenue, after every cost. |
| **Contribution %** | What the work itself made as a share of revenue, with time not on a job left out. |
| **Labor % of revenue** | Labor cost as a share of revenue. Lower is better. |
| **Units** | Good units logged. Scrap is left out. |
| **Labor hours** | People times hours each. |
| **Units per hour** | All good units over all labor hours. It can differ a little from a job's pace, which leaves out setup hours and days waiting for hours. |
| **Cost per unit** | All cost over good units. |

## Drill down

1. Click a row, for example a client.
2. The page goes one level down, to that client's jobs. Click a job to see its days.
3. **Where you are** shows the path. Remove a step to go back up.

At the lowest level, open the entries behind a number to see each row: day, job, what it was, units, hours and amount.

## Job margin and company margin

The top of the page shows **Job margin** and **Company margin** side by side. Select **How we got company profit** to see each step from profit on jobs to company profit. A button switches how company costs are shared for a part of a month: **Share company costs by work day** or **Share company costs by calendar day**. See [How Pulse does the math](/docs/pulse/how-pulse-does-the-math).

## Save a view and export

- Open **Saved views** and select **Save this view**. It keeps the grouping, period, filters, sort and columns. Turn on **Share with the workspace** so everyone who can open Profit sees it.
- Export the table as a CSV file. A chart export holds the numbers behind the chart, as the table shows them.
