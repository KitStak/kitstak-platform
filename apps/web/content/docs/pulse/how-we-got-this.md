Money figures and margins in Pulse show their work. Next to a number, select the small **How we got this** button. A panel opens with the rule in plain words, the same rule with your own figures, a link to the rows behind the number, and a warning line whenever the number is not complete.

A number with no warning line is complete.

## Open it

1. Find the number: on Home, a job page, a client page, Profit, Labor, the log grid or the calculator.
2. Select **How we got this** next to it.
3. Read the panel. Select a link such as **See 14 entries**, **See the entries** or **See where paid time went** to open the rows behind it.

## What the panel holds

| Part | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| The rule | The formula in words, for example "Labor hours x the real cost of an hour". |
| Your figures | The same formula with the numbers, step by step, ending on the total. |
| Links | The entries or the page the number comes from. |
| **Things that make this number partial** | Each gap that makes the number too high or too low. |

People who do not see money get the same words with every money figure left out: **Money is hidden for your role. Ask an admin if you need it.**

## Warning lines you may see

| Line | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Days have units and no hours, so labor cost is too low. | Add the people and hours on those days. |
| Entries have no labor rate, so those hours cost nothing here. | Add a rate for the labor class. See [Set labor classes and rates](/docs/pulse/labor-rates). |
| Jobs have no price, so revenue is too low. | Set a price on the job. See [Set prices and track revenue](/docs/pulse/prices-and-revenue). |
| Extra service entries have no price. | Set the price. See [Set up and track extra services](/docs/pulse/extra-services). |
| Entries use the default rate because no labor class was picked. | Pick a labor class on those entries. |
| Days have more hours logged than paid. | Check the paid hours and the entries. |
| No labor hours or parts are logged for this work. | The margin shows once a cost is logged. |
| Company costs for part of a month | Pulse names the days counted, for example "Company costs for 18 of 30 days." |

## Numbers Pulse can explain

Revenue, labor, material, other cost, time not on a job, cost, profit, job margin, the margin before time not on a job, labor as a share of revenue, units per person per hour, cost per unit, units, hours, company profit and not billed yet.

## Worked example (made-up figures)

A job's labor cost reads $2,875.00. **How we got this** shows:

- In words: labor hours x the real cost of an hour.
- With your figures: 125 labor hours x $23.00 an hour = $2,875.00.
- A link: **See 9 entries**.
- No warning line, so the number is complete.
