The wall display is a full screen view for the floor TV: large type, no menus, and panels that rotate on their own. It shows each job's units against today's goal and the crew's pace against the target, and it refreshes every minute. A TV link opens it with no login and never shows money.

## Before you start

- Anyone with a Pulse role can open the wall in their own browser.
- Making a TV link needs a manager, admin or owner.

## Open the wall

1. Search for **Open wall display**, or select **Wall display** on the Goals page.
2. If you run more than one site, pick the site.
3. Select **Full screen**. If the browser blocks it, try the `F11` key.

People who see money can turn on **Show money** to add what was earned today. It is off by default.

## The panels

| Panel | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Units against the goal** | Each job's units today against today's goal. |
| **Pace against the target** | Each job's units per person per hour against its target pace. |
| **The day's totals** | **Units done**, **People on jobs**, **Jobs with work logged** and, with money on, **Earned**. |
| **Due this week** | Jobs due this week. |
| **Nice work** | Jobs beating their target pace. A job with missing hours never shows here. |

Each job gets a word: **On pace**, **A bit slow**, **Behind** or **Waiting** (nothing logged yet). The lines are the same as on the log grid, from **Settings > Cost rules**. Use **Screen before**, **Next screen** and **Stop rotating** to move by hand.

## Today's goal for a job

1. When the job has a usual crew and a target pace: usual crew x shift hours x target pace.
2. Else, when it has a due date: units left divided by the work days left to the due date, today included.
3. Else there is no goal, and the panel says **No goal today: no usual crew, target pace or due date.**

Goals are rounded up to a whole unit.

## Put the wall on a TV

1. On the wall, select **TV link**.
2. In **Put the wall on a TV**, type the **Name of the screen**, like "Line 2 TV".
3. Pick how long **Each screen stays up for**: 10, 15, 20, 30 or 60 seconds.
4. Pick the **Site**, or tick **Show every site on this TV**.
5. Tick the **Screens to rotate through**.
6. Turn on **Hide client names** for floors where visitors can see the screen.
7. Select **Make a new link**.
8. Copy the link and open it in the browser of the TV. For safety it is shown only this once.

A TV link never shows money, and you can turn it off at any time. When a link is off or has run out, the TV shows **This wall link is not active**. TV links also show under **Settings > Device links > Wall displays**.

## Worked example (made-up figures)

A job's usual crew is 6 people, the shift is 8 hours and the target pace is 40 kits per person per hour. Today's goal: 6 x 8 x 40 = 1,920 kits.
