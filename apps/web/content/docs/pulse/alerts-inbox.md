Pulse watches every open job. When one slips, for example the margin is heading under your target or a day was not logged, an alert lands in the **Alerts** inbox with what happened, why, and what to do next. The number on the **Alerts** link counts what needs you.

## Before you start

- Everyone with a Pulse role can read alerts. Changing them needs a role that may manage alerts.
- Which alerts exist depends on the rules. See [Set alert rules](/docs/pulse/alert-rules).

## The tabs

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs you** | Every open alert. |
| **Assigned to me** | Alerts a manager handed to you. |
| **Snoozed** | Alerts waiting to come back on a day you picked. |
| **Done** | Alerts marked done or dismissed, next to the ones that cleared on their own when the problem went away. |

Search with a job number, a client name or one word of what happened.

## Read an alert

Each alert shows:

| Part | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| How urgent | **Urgent**, **Heads up** or **Good to know**. Urgent alerts sort to the top. |
| **What happened** | The problem in plain words. |
| **Why:** | The reason, with the numbers. |
| **Next step:** | What to do, with a link straight to the job. |
| **Money at stake** | How much profit the problem is costing right now, from the same numbers the job page and Home show. Shown to people who see money. |
| **Assigned to** | Who it was handed to. |
| **First seen**, **Last checked** | When it fired and when Pulse last looked. |

## Act on an alert

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Snooze** | It leaves the inbox and comes back on the day you pick, if the problem is still there. Pick **Until tomorrow**, **For 3 days**, **Until next Monday**, **For 2 weeks**, or **Pick a day**. |
| **Assign** | Hands the alert to someone. They see it under **Assigned to me** and get a message. |
| **Mark done** | You dealt with it. |
| **Dismiss** | **Dismiss, this is fine**: the alert stays quiet while nothing changes. |
| **Open again** or **Bring back now** | Puts a done or snoozed alert back in the inbox. |

To act on many at once, tick them, or tick the header box to pick all, then use **Change many alerts at once**: snooze, assign, mark done, dismiss or open again. Every change has an undo.

## Check now

Pulse checks the rules on its own every 15 minutes. Select **Check now** to run them at once. It says how many new alerts came in and how many cleared on their own.

## Alerts that clear on their own

When the problem goes away, for example the missing hours are added, the next check clears the alert and moves it to **Done** as **Cleared on its own**. Its money figure then shows what the problem was costing when the alert fired.

## Your daily summary

Under **Settings > Notifications**, **One summary a day** leaves one note a day under the bell with what to look at first, when something needs you.
