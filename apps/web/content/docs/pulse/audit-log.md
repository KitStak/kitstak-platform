Every change in Pulse is written down: who did it, when, and what the value was before and after. Nothing in the audit log can be edited or deleted. Use it to answer "who changed this price?" or "why did this month's profit move?".

## Before you start

- Go to **More > Settings** and open the **Audit log** tab.
- People who do not see money see the log with money values left out.
- Each job also has its own history on its **Activity** tab. See [Work with a job page](/docs/pulse/job-page).

## Find a change

1. Type in **Search the audit log**: a job number, a name or a value.
2. Narrow the list with the filters in the table below.
3. Select **Show the before and after** on a row to see each field's old and new value. **Show before and after** opens them on every row.

| Filter | What it narrows |
| --- | --- |
| **Who made the change** | One person. |
| **What kind of change** | **Jobs**, **Production entries**, **Prices**, **Quotes and budgets**, **Labor rates**, **Costs**, **Revenue**, **Materials**, **Day reviews**, **Month close**, **Imports**, **Exports**, **Settings**, **Roles**, **Who sees money**, **Sample data** or **Deleted data**. |
| Kind of record | One kind of record. |
| **From date** and **To date** | A date range. |

## Export the log

Select **Export CSV**. The file holds the rows that match your filters.

## What the log records

- Every save, delete and restore of a job, entry, price, plan, rate, cost, invoice or material row.
- Imports and their undo, and every export.
- Day reviews: marked complete, approved, sent back.
- Month close and reopen, with the reason.
- Settings, role and money access changes.
- Sample data loaded or removed, and all data deleted.
