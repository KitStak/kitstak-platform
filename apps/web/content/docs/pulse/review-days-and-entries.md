Once the floor marks a day complete, a manager checks it before the numbers are used. Pulse points out the rows that look off. The **All entries** tab is where anyone finds an entry across days and jobs, and where a manager fixes many at once.

## Before you start

- **Review the day** needs a manager, admin or owner.
- **All entries** is open to every role. Changing entries needs a role that may change them.

## Review a day

1. Go to **Log work** and open **Review the day**.
2. Pick **To review** or **Approved**, and the site if you have more than one. The list covers the last 21 days, or pick a month.
3. Open a day. Each day reads **Ready for review**, **Not marked complete** or **Sent back**.
4. Read **Rows that look odd**. Select **Fix rows here** to correct a row without leaving the page.
5. Select **Approve** when the day is right.
6. Or select **Send back**, type **What needs fixing**, and confirm. The floor can change the day again and sees your note at the top.

## What counts as odd

| Flag | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Units with no hours | A job shows units but no labor hours. Add the people and hours. |
| Pace under half the target | Check the hours and the units. |
| Pace over double the target | Look for a typo. |
| More hours logged than were paid | Check the paid hours and the entries. |
| Ran the day before, nothing today | A job ran the work day before but has nothing logged here. |

An approved day can still be changed by a manager, and the numbers update right away. **Open the day again** on Log work reopens a day that was marked complete.

## Find an entry

1. Go to **Log work** and open **All entries**.
2. Narrow the list with **From day**, **To day**, the site, **Labor class**, **Where the entry came from**, and **Search job or note**.
3. Turn on **Needs hours** to see only rows with units and no hours.

Each row shows the day, job, labor class, units good, people, labor hours, labor cost, source and note. Rows in a closed month show **Closed**.

## Fix many entries at once

1. Tick the entries, or **Choose every entry on this page**.
2. To move them to another labor class, pick it under **New labor class for the chosen entries** and select **Set labor class**. Labor cost is worked out again with the new real cost of an hour.
3. To remove them, use the remove action. Select **Put back** on the toast to undo.

To edit one day, select its edit button. The edit form has **Hours each**, **Overtime hours** (part of the labor hours) and **Setup hours** (do not count in the pace). Undo puts the day back the way it was, unless it was changed again after your save.

## Export entries

Export the filtered list as a CSV file. The file can be changed in a spreadsheet and imported back with the **Production** template.
