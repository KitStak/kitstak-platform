**Reports** holds 19 ready made reports. Each one has filters, a chart, a table, a print view and a CSV download, and each can be built for you on a schedule. Week based reports follow the week start in your settings.

## Before you start

- Supervisors do not see Reports. Reports about money need a role that sees money: other roles see **Shows money. Ask an admin if you need it.**

## Run a report

1. Go to **Reports**. The **Library** tab lists every report, grouped.
2. Select a report.
3. Pick the period and filters at the top. They stay in the page address, so you can bookmark the report as you set it.
4. Read the summary, the chart and the table. Click a column name to sort. The download keeps the order the report was built in.
5. Select **Print** for a print view, or **Download CSV** for the file.

A long report shows its first 5,000 rows on screen. Narrow the dates to see the rest.

## The reports

### Profit

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Job profit** | Revenue, cost, profit and margin for every job. |
| **Profit by client** | Which clients make you money and which ones cost you. |
| **Profit by job type** | Which kinds of work pay best. |
| **Profit by service** | Revenue, cost and margin for each extra service. |
| **Profit by site** | Your sites side by side: margin, pace and time not on a job. |
| **Monthly profit and loss** | Each month from job profit down to company profit. |
| **Cost to serve** | What it really costs to serve each client, per unit and per job. |

### Plan and risk

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan vs actual** | What you planned next to what happened, for each job. |
| **Quote vs actual** | How each job is doing against the price you quoted. |
| **Open jobs at risk** | Open jobs that are at risk or losing money, worst first, with the reason. |
| **Why profit changed** | What moved profit away from the plan: price, pace, rates, materials or volume. |

### Work on the floor

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Pace vs target** | Units per person per hour against the target pace, by job and day. Shows no money. |
| **Labor summary** | Hours and labor cost by labor class, job type and client. |
| **Paid time not on any job** | Hours you paid for that nobody logged to a job or a reason. Shows no money. |
| **Material and scrap** | Parts used, parts scrapped and what the scrap cost. |
| **Daily production log** | Every production entry: units, people, hours and notes. Shows no money. |

### Billing and month end

| Report | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to bill** | Work that is done and earned but not billed yet, by client and job. Hand it to accounting. |
| **Month close pack** | Everything finance needs to close the month in one place: revenue, profit on jobs, company profit and whether the month is ready to close. |
| **Audit log** | Who changed what, and when, with the before and after. Shows no money. |
