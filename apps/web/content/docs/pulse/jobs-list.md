The **Jobs** page lists every job, with the ones at risk or losing money first. Use it each morning to see which open jobs need you, and why. The number on the **Jobs** link in the top bar counts the open jobs at risk or losing money.

## Before you start

- Everyone with a Pulse role can read the list. Adding, changing and bulk actions need a manager.
- Margin and profit columns show only to people who see money. See [Roles and who sees money](/docs/pulse/roles-and-access).

## The tabs

Go to **Jobs**. The tabs across the top are:

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Active and on hold jobs. This is the default tab, sorted worst first. |
| **All** | Every job, whatever its stage. |
| **Completed** | Finished jobs with their final revenue, cost and margin. |
| **Planned** | Jobs quoted or booked but not started. |
| **Deleted** | Deleted jobs waiting to be restored. Managers only. See [Work with a job page](/docs/pulse/job-page). |

## Filter by status

Above the list, one chip per status shows how many jobs have it. Select a chip to show only those jobs. Select more than one to combine them. The chips are, in order:

1. **Losing money**
2. **At risk**
3. **Not enough data**
4. **Heads up**
5. **On track**

What each status means, and the exact rules, are in [How Pulse judges a job](/docs/pulse/job-status).

## Search and filters

- **Search by job, client or PO** finds a job by its number, name, client or client PO number.
- Filters: client, **Job type**, site, **Due from** and **Due to**, **Fed by a program** (jobs a KitStak Projects program feeds) and **Show archived jobs**.

## Quick add a job

The **Quick add a job** row at the top is the fast way in.

1. Pick the client, or type a new client name.
2. Type the job name, the **Units to make** and the **Price each**.
3. Press `Enter`.

The job starts today with the General job type, and its quote is made from the price. If the client was new, Pulse adds it too. Select **More options** for the full new job page. See [Add a job](/docs/pulse/add-a-job).

## Sort, group and pick columns

| Control | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Sort by** | **Worst first**, **Profit at finish**, **Margin now**, **Due date**, **Share done**, **Last entry**, **Client name**, **Job name**, **Job number**. |
| **Group rows by** | **No grouping**, **Status**, **Client**, **Job type**, **Site**, **Owner**, **Stage**, **Program**. |
| **Show columns** | **Client**, **Job type**, **Site**, **Owner**, **Stage**, **Status and reason**, **Units done**, **Labor hours**, **Margin now**, **Margin at finish**, **Profit at finish**, **Trend**, **Due**, **Last entry**. |

The two layout buttons switch between **List** and **Board**. The board has one column per status, worst on the left. Pulse places each job from its numbers, so you cannot drag a card: fix the cause and the card moves itself.

## Save a view

1. Set the search, filters, sort, grouping, columns and layout you want.
2. Open **Views** and select **Save this view**.
3. Give the view a name. Turn on **Share with everyone in this workspace** to share it.

A view can be updated, renamed or deleted from its options menu. A deleted view can be brought back with **Undo**.

## Bulk actions

1. Tick the jobs you want, or tick the box in the header to pick every job in the list.
2. Open **Bulk actions** and pick **Set stage**, **Set owner**, **Archive** or **Export these**.

Every bulk change shows an undo toast.

## Export the list

Select **Export CSV**. The file holds the rows and columns on screen.
