KitStak Pulse is profit management for shops that work by the job: kitting, 3PL and contract packaging. You log units, people and hours each day. Pulse turns them into revenue, cost, profit and margin for every job, client, job type and extra service, and tells you in plain words which open jobs need you.

Pulse runs on its own. Every kind of data can come in and go out as a CSV file. If you also use KitStak CRM, WMS or Projects, Pulse reads what they know. See [Connected apps](/docs/pulse/connected-apps).

## The words Pulse uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Job** | One piece of work for one client, like building 5,000 holiday gift kits. One job has one kind of unit and one price. |
| **Revenue** | What the work earned: units finished times the price, plus extra services, plus fees, less credits. Invoices never change it. |
| **Labor cost** | Labor hours times the real cost of an hour. |
| **Material cost** | Parts and supplies you pay for, scrap included. Parts the client supplies cost nothing. |
| **Other cost** | Freight, packaging, outside help and the like. |
| **Time not on a job** | Meetings, cleanup and waiting. Paid time that is shared across the jobs that ran. |
| **Profit** | Revenue less every cost. |
| **Job margin** | Profit on jobs as a share of revenue. |
| **Company margin** | Company profit as a share of revenue. Company profit also takes away costs no job carried. |
| **Real cost of an hour** | The wage, plus payroll taxes and benefits, plus overhead for each hour. |
| **Target pace** | How many units one person should finish in an hour. |
| **Quote** | What you told the client. |
| **Budget** | What you think the job will cost you. |
| **Actual** | What happened. |

## Find your way around

The top bar holds the main pages. If the bar is too narrow, the last links move into **More**. On a small screen the links sit in a menu.

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | How the business is doing and which jobs need you. |
| **Jobs** | Every job, with the ones at risk or losing money first. |
| **Log work** | Type in what got finished today: units, people and hours. |
| **Profit** | Revenue, cost and margin by job, client, job type, service, site or month. |
| **Labor** | What an hour really costs, where paid time goes, and labor cost. |
| **Alerts** | Things that need you, with what happened and the next step. |
| **Reports** | Ready made reports you can filter, print, export or build on a schedule. |
| **More > Target pace** | How many units one person should finish in an hour. |
| **More > Calculator** | Price a new job or test a what if before you say yes. |
| **More > Goals** | Targets for margin, profit and pace, and how you are doing against them. |
| **More > Import and export** | Bring in spreadsheets and download your data as CSV files. |
| **More > Settings** | Workspace settings, people, month close, connected apps and the audit log. |

What you see depends on your Pulse role. Supervisors land on **Log work** and do not see Home, Profit, Reports, Calculator, Goals or Settings. See [Roles and who sees money](/docs/pulse/roles-and-access).

## Search and keyboard shortcuts

Press `Ctrl` `K` (or `Cmd` `K` on a Mac), or `/`, to open search and commands. Type a job number, a client name or a page name. The list also has actions such as **Log work**, **New job**, **Log many days**, **Review the day**, **Import a file**, **Export data**, **Open wall display** and **Close a month**.

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `?` | Show the shortcut sheet. |
| `l` | Open Log work. |
| `n` | Start a new job. |
| `g` then `h` | Go to Home. |
| `g` then `j` | Go to Jobs. |
| `g` then `l` | Go to Log work. |
| `g` then `p` | Go to Profit. |
| `g` then `b` | Go to Labor. |
| `g` then `a` | Go to Alerts. |
| `g` then `r` | Go to Reports. |
| `g` then `t` | Go to Target pace. |
| `g` then `c` | Go to Calculator. |
| `g` then `o` | Go to Goals. |
| `g` then `i` | Go to Import and export. |
| `g` then `s` | Go to Settings. |

A shortcut only works when your role can open that page.
