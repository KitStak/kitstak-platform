A client is a company you do work for. Every job belongs to one client, so profit adds up by client. You add clients in Settings, and you read each client's numbers on its own page, reached from the **Clients** tab of Profit.

## Before you start

- Adding and editing clients needs a manager, admin or owner, in **Settings > Catalogs**. You can also add a client while adding a job: type a new name and Pulse adds it.
- Client money shows only to people who see money.

## Add a client

1. Go to **More > Settings** and open **Catalogs**.
2. On the **Clients** tab, select **Add a client**.
3. Fill in the fields and select **Add client**.

| Field | What to type |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The company name, like Acme Outdoor Co. |
| **Short code** | Used in files and reports. Leave it empty and Pulse makes one. |
| **Contact name**, **Contact email**, **Phone** | Who you deal with. |
| **Status** | Active or not active. |
| **Target margin** | The margin you aim for with this client. Empty uses the job type or workspace target. |
| **Days to pay** | Like 30 for net 30. |
| **Who buys the parts** | **Follow the company rule**, **We do. Parts are our cost** or **The client does. Parts cost us nothing**. |
| **Notes** | Anything the team should know. |

If the name looks like a client you already have, Pulse asks first, so you do not end up with two of the same client.

## Open a client

1. Go to **Profit** and open the **Clients** tab.
2. Select a client name. The client page opens.

Pick the period at the top. Every figure follows it.

| Part of the page | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Tiles | Revenue, cost, profit, **Job margin**, **Labor as % of revenue**, **Not billed yet** and **Cost to serve**. |
| **Trend** | Revenue and cost bars with the job margin line next to the client's target margin. |
| **Cost to serve** | Every cost of the client's jobs, plus costs put on the client. Shown **Per unit**, **Per job** and as a share of revenue, with a split of labor, material, other job costs, time not on a job and costs on the client. |
| **Jobs** | Each job's revenue, cost, profit, margin and units. Revenue and costs that sit on the client with no job show as **Put on the client, no job**. |
| **Extra services** | What this client buys on top of the jobs. |
| **About this client** | Target margin, who buys the parts, payment terms, contact, how many jobs in all and open, and labor hours in the period. |
| **Last changes** | Who changed what on this client and its jobs. |

To change the target margin from the client page, select **Change the target margin**, type a percent from 0 to 99, and select **Save the target margin**. Leave it blank to use the workspace target. To start a job for this client, select **New job**.

## Worked example (made-up figures)

A client has two jobs in October with $9,000.00 of job cost on 6,000 units, plus a $600.00 storage cost put on the client. Cost to serve is $9,600.00: $1.60 a unit and $4,800.00 a job.
