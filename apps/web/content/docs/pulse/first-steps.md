The **Getting started** page walks you through five short steps, from an empty workspace to a real profit number on a job. Most people finish in under ten minutes. Use round numbers for now: you can fix anything later.

Open it from search (`Ctrl` `K`, then **Getting started**). Until you finish, Home shows the same **Five steps to your first profit number** list.

## Before you start

- The first step sets a labor rate, which holds wages, so it needs an admin or the owner. See [Roles and who sees money](/docs/pulse/roles-and-access).
- Adding a client and a job needs a manager. Logging a day needs a supervisor or a manager.

## Who runs Pulse

When nobody runs Pulse for your workspace yet, a banner reads **Nobody runs Pulse for this workspace yet**. The person who runs Pulse sets labor rates, decides who can see money and closes months.

1. Select **Run Pulse for this workspace**.
2. Pulse confirms with **You now run Pulse for this workspace**. You are now the Pulse owner.

## Step 1: Set a labor rate

1. In **Set a labor rate**, type the **Hourly wage of a packer**, like 16.00.
2. Pulse shows what that hour really costs once the payroll taxes and benefits from your labor defaults are added.
3. Select **Save the rate**.

You can add overhead and more labor classes later under **Labor > Rates**. See [Set labor classes and rates](/docs/pulse/labor-rates).

## Step 2: Add a client

1. In **Add a client**, type the **Client name**, like Acme Outdoor Co.
2. Select **Add the client**. If a client with a close name exists, Pulse offers it, or you can pick **Add as a new client**.

## Step 3: Add a job

1. In **Add a job**, type the job name, the **Units to build** and the **Price for one**.
2. Select **Add the job**.

For more fields, select **More options** to open the full new job page. See [Add a job](/docs/pulse/add-a-job).

## Step 4: Log a day

1. In **Log a day**, type the units finished today, the **People** and the **Hours each**.
2. Pulse shows the labor hours: people times hours each.
3. Select **Log the day**.

## Step 5: See your profit

As soon as the day is logged, **Your first profit number** shows the profit on the job so far and its margin. Select **How we got this profit** to see the math, or **Open the job** to see the job page.

If the job has no price yet, the step says so. Give it a price for one unit on the job page.

## Other ways to start

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sample data** | Loads five sample jobs with two weeks of logged work, three labor rates, materials, extra services, costs and invoices. One job is healthy and one is heading for a loss. Every sample name ends in "(sample)". Needs an admin. |
| **Import spreadsheets** | Opens Import and export, where every kind of data has a template. |
| **Use your KitStak apps** | Shown when your workspace has CRM, WMS or Projects. Opens Connected apps. |

To remove sample data later, go to **Settings > Sample data** and select **Remove sample data**. Your own jobs and entries stay as they are.
