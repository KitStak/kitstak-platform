A goal is a number to aim for, like a 30 percent margin or 40 units per person per hour. Set one for the whole company, a site, a client, a job type or an extra service. Pulse shows how you are doing against it each month, and for money goals, whether you are on pace by today.

Most teams start with one margin goal for the whole company.

## Before you start

- Setting goals needs a manager, admin or owner. Everyone else sees progress against them.
- Money goals show no numbers to roles that do not see money.

## Add a goal

1. Go to **More > Goals** and select **Add a goal**.
2. Under **Who is it for**, pick **Whole company**, **Site**, **Client**, **Job type** or **Extra service**, then which one.
3. Under **What to measure**, pick the measure and type the number.
4. Under **When the goal counts**, pick **Every month** or one month only. A goal for one month wins over the every month goal for that month.
5. Select **Add the goal**.

| Measure | What it is | Better when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Margin** | Profit as a share of revenue, after all costs. | Higher |
| **Profit** | Revenue less every cost, added up over the month. | Higher |
| **Revenue** | What the work earned, added up over the month. | Higher |
| **Units per person per hour** | Good units divided by labor hours. Not for extra services. | Higher |
| **Labor as a share of revenue** | Labor cost divided by revenue. | Lower |
| **Cost per unit** | All cost divided by good units. Not for extra services. | Lower |
| **Paid time spent on jobs** | Hours logged on jobs divided by paid hours. Company and sites only. | Higher |

## Read the board

The top of the page, **This month at a glance**, says which work day of the month it is, for example "Work day 12 of 21 in November". Use **Month before** and **Month after** to move between months. The goals are grouped **Whole company**, **By site**, **By client**, **By job type** and **By extra service**.

Each goal shows its status, the actual next to the goal, and a trend over the last 6 months.

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| **On pace** | At or past the goal. |
| **Close** | Within 10 percent of the line. |
| **Behind** | More than 10 percent short. |
| **No numbers yet** | Nothing logged for it yet, or the month has not started. |

## Money goals are checked against the pace

Profit and revenue add up over the month, so Pulse spreads the goal evenly over the work days of the month. By today you should be at that share of the goal. Pulse also shows where the month ends if the days left go like the days so far. The other measures are compared straight to the goal.

## Worked example (made-up figures)

Your November profit goal is $42,000.00. November has 21 work days, and today is work day 12.

- By today you should be at $42,000.00 x 12 / 21 = $24,000.00.
- You are at $22,800.00, which is 95% of where you should be: **Close**.
- At this pace the month ends near $22,800.00 x 21 / 12 = $39,900.00.

## Import and export goals

Use the **Goals** template to bring goals in. The export button on the Goals page downloads them. See [CSV files and their columns](/docs/pulse/csv-templates).
