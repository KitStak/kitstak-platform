> **Note:** Crew is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

Crew is planned to compare each person's punches with their schedule, so attendance records itself. Supervisors see who is here, who is late and who is missing without walking the floor with a clipboard.

## What Crew records

| Event | When it is planned to count |
| --- | --- |
| Late | The person clocks in after their shift starts. |
| Early out | The person clocks out before their shift ends. |
| No call no show | The person was scheduled, did not clock in, and did not call in. |
| Called out | The person told a supervisor they would miss the shift. |
| Time off | An approved request covers the shift. |

## An attendance policy

Many shops give points or occurrences for late arrivals and absences. Crew is planned to support a policy your organization sets. Whether a points policy is in the first release, or only the raw late and absent record, is still being decided.

## Time off

- People ask for time off, and a supervisor approves or declines it.
- Balances show how much paid time off each person has left.
- Approved time off shows on the schedule, so nobody gets scheduled while they are out.
- If you use Projects, time off shows in workload too.

## Who is here today

A daily board for each site is planned to show:

- Who is clocked in now, and in which area.
- Who is scheduled and not here yet.
- Who is out on time off or called out.

That board is also what WMS reads to know who is on site. See [How Crew connects to the other apps](/docs/crew/connected-apps).
