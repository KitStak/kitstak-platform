> **Note:** Crew is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

Some work needs a card or training before anyone does it. Crew is planned to keep track of who is cleared for what, and to warn you before you put the wrong person on the wrong job.

## Certifications

A certification is a card or training with an end date. Examples:

- Forklift.
- Reach truck.
- Hazmat handling.

Each one is planned to hold the date earned, the date it ends, and a copy of the card if you want one. Crew warns you before a card runs out, so there is time to renew it.

## Trained on

Trained on is the work a person knows how to do, even when no card is involved:

- A kit, like a holiday gift set build.
- A line, like kitting line 2.
- A client's program, when that client has its own rules.

## Skill level

Each skill is planned to have a level, from in training to able to train others. Leads can use it to pair new people with experienced ones.

## Warnings when you schedule

When you put someone on a shift or an area, Crew is planned to check their skills and warn you when:

- The work needs a certification they do not have.
- Their certification has run out or will run out before the shift.
- They are not trained on the kit, line or program.

If you use WMS, it is planned to read the same record, so task assignment can skip people who are not cleared. No forklift putaway goes to someone whose card ran out.
