> **Note:** Crew is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

The schedule is where you decide who works when. Crew is planned to build it by week, for each site and area, from shift templates you set up once.

## Shift templates

A template is a shift you run again and again, like first shift, second shift or a weekend shift. It holds:

- The start and end time.
- Paid and unpaid breaks.
- The site and, if you want, the area, like kitting line 2 or receiving.

You build a week by placing templates on days, then putting people on them.

## Build the week

The plan for the schedule:

- See the week by site and area, with each person's shifts in a row.
- Copy last week as a starting point.
- See the labor plan beside the schedule, so you know how many people each day needs. See [Labor planning](/docs/crew/labor-planning).
- Get a warning when someone is on time off, already near overtime, or not cleared for the work. See [Skills and certifications](/docs/crew/skills-and-certifications).

## Open shifts, swaps and pickups

- An open shift has no one on it yet. People who can work it see it and can ask for it.
- A swap trades a shift between two people. A supervisor approves it.
- A pickup is someone taking an open shift or a shift another person gave up.

## Publish and notify

A draft schedule is only visible to the people building it. When you publish the week, everyone on it is planned to get a notice with their shifts. If you change a published shift, the people on it hear about the change.
