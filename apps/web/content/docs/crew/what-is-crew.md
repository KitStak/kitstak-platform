> **Note:** Crew is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

KitStak Crew is the planned people app for kitting, 3PL and contract packaging floors. It keeps a record of everyone who works the floor, builds the weekly schedule, runs a time clock at the door, tracks attendance and skills, and tells you how many people the planned work needs.

Labor is the biggest cost on most kitting floors. The other KitStak apps already know what the work is and what an hour costs. Crew adds who showed up, for how long, and who you will need next.

## The words Crew uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Person | Anyone who works the floor: an employee, a temp from an agency or a seasonal hire. A person does not need a KitStak sign in. |
| User | Someone who signs in to KitStak. A person can also be a user, like a lead who approves time. |
| Agency | A temp staffing company that sends people to your floor. |
| Shift | A block of work on a day, at a site, in an area. |
| Punch | One clock in, clock out, break or switch on the time clock. |
| Kiosk | A shared tablet at the door where people clock in and out. |
| Labor class | The kind of work a person is paid for, like packer or lead. |
| Certification | A card or training with an end date, like forklift or hazmat. |
| Labor plan | Hours and people the planned work needs, compared with who is scheduled. |

## What Crew is planned to cover

- [People, users, temps and agencies](/docs/crew/people): everyone on the floor, signed in or not.
- [Shifts and the schedule](/docs/crew/shifts-and-schedule): shift templates, the weekly schedule, open shifts and swaps.
- [The time clock and kiosk](/docs/crew/time-clock-and-kiosk): clock in at the door or by phone, with breaks and job switches.
- [Approve hours and send them to payroll](/docs/crew/approve-hours): supervisor edits with a reason, then a locked week.
- [Attendance and time off](/docs/crew/attendance-and-time-off): late, absent, time off requests and the daily board.
- [Skills and certifications](/docs/crew/skills-and-certifications): who is cleared for what, and warnings before you schedule someone who is not.
- [Labor planning](/docs/crew/labor-planning): the people tomorrow needs, and the gap.
- [How Crew connects to the other apps](/docs/crew/connected-apps): Pulse, Ledger, WMS, Projects and CRM.

## What Crew will not do at first

- Run payroll. Crew approves hours and sends them to your payroll provider.
- Recruit or track applicants. A person starts in Crew once they are hired.
- Hold benefits or HR case files. Crew is a floor tool, not an HR suite.
- Keep a second set of pay rates. If you use Pulse, labor classes and rates stay in Pulse.

## Works on its own

A shop with only Crew is planned to get scheduling, a time clock, attendance and skills, and to send hours to payroll. Adding Pulse later turns those hours into job cost with no file in between. Adding Projects or WMS later feeds the labor plan.
