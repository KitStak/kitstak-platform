> **Note:** Crew is planned and not available yet. This page describes how it is planned to work, and details may change before launch.

Punches are evidence of what people worked, so Crew is planned to treat every change to them with care. A supervisor fixes what needs fixing, approves the week, and the approved hours go to payroll.

## Fix a punch

People forget to clock out, or clock in at the wrong site. A supervisor is planned to be able to:

- Add a missing punch.
- Change the time of a punch.
- Move a punch to the right job or area.

Every edit needs a reason. Crew keeps the original punch, who changed it, when and why. Nothing is overwritten.

## Approve the week

When the week's punches look right, a supervisor approves them. Approved hours are split into regular and overtime for each person, using the overtime rules for their site.

An approved week locks, the same way Pulse locks a closed month. To change a locked week, someone reopens it with a reason, and the reopen is logged.

## Send hours to payroll

Crew does not run payroll. It is planned to send approved hours to your payroll provider. Providers like Gusto, ADP, Paychex and QuickBooks Payroll are on the list. Which ones Crew connects to first is still being decided.

## Where else approved hours go

- Pulse, as paid hours for each person, day, site and labor class. That fills the payroll check without a time punch file.
- Ledger, as hours per agency per week, to check temp agency bills.

See [How Crew connects to the other apps](/docs/crew/connected-apps).
